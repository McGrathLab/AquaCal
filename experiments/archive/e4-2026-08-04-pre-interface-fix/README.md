# E4 baseline — archived 2026-08-04, before the single-flat-interface fix

Byte-for-byte copies of E4's two manuscript-facing files, plus recorded
pointers to the nine per-cell provenance records, as they stood at commit
`22e75ef2b424c9b0234502e5229345a9f5912b11`, taken BEFORE Phase 19.4's
single-flat-interface fix.

## Why this exists

`generate_camera_array` (`src/aquacal/datasets/synthetic.py:255-260`) applied
its `height_variation` jitter to `water_z` — the world-frame Z of the water
*surface*, shared by every camera per the paper's whole premise — while
holding every camera at `C_z = 0`. That gave the synthetic ground truth one
water surface PER CAMERA instead of one shared plane. Measured over 31,680
corner observations (one seed, one trajectory — see the caveat below): mean
displacement **1.42 px**, max **6.33 px**, against an E4/E6 reprojection RMS
of only ~0.4–0.9 px. The modelling error exceeded the residual being
reported. D-19.4-09 moves the jitter from `water_z` to `C_z`, so `h_c =
water_z - C_z` (the physical camera-to-water gap) is preserved exactly per
camera while every camera now looks through the SAME surface.

E4 is directly affected because it is a `generate_camera_array` consumer
(via `build_grid_scenario`), and because `GRID_DEPTH_RANGE` — the frozen
module-level depth range every grid cell inherits — is derived from
`_grid_baseline_water_zs`, which moves under this fix (see D-19.4-15 for the
paired E6 re-derivation; E4 itself does not re-derive a constant, but its
cells run against geometry generated by the corrected function).

**This is ONE seed, ONE trajectory.** The 1.42 px / 6.33 px figures are
enough to establish that the defect is real and dominant — they are NOT an
effect size, and must not be quoted as one anywhere near the manuscript
(blocking anti-pattern 7, `19.4-CONTEXT.md`).

## Why it is NOT under `experiments/results/`

`tests/unit/test_experiments_provenance.py` globs `experiments/results/` at
collection time and asserts every discovered artifact carries its provenance
record. Archived copies placed there would be swept into that gate as if
they were live results. This directory is deliberately outside that glob.

## How to use it

After the single-flat-interface re-run, diff the regenerated
`benchmark_grid.csv` against the copy here. Both a moved reprojection number
AND a moved optimality/degenerate-guard-count are expected consequences of
this fix. E4 has no measured seed band (D-19.4-13's Deferred Ideas), so do
not claim a quantified accuracy delta from this diff alone — report the
direction and magnitude of movement, not a precision claim beyond what one
seed supports. Do not delete this directory until the manuscript's numbers
have been reconciled against the re-run.
