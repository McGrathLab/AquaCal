# E6 baseline — archived 2026-08-02, before the scenario depth-clearance fix

A byte-for-byte copy of E6's manuscript-facing CSV, plus recorded pointers
to the run-level and per-configuration provenance records, as they stood at
commit `74e75a7b33d4d9be1467f5806e76d1e77604e047`, taken BEFORE Phase
19.3's synthetic scenario depth-clearance fix.

## Why this exists

Every synthetic scenario generated by `generate_board_trajectory` /
`generate_real_rig_trajectory` allowed board corners to protrude through the
water surface. That put the residual on the refractive/pinhole hinge in
`compute_residuals` (the pinhole continuation guard added to fix the
`7e0cb90` absorbing-region defect), which made first-order optimality
unreadable as a convergence diagnostic for every affected experiment
(`.planning/phases/19.3-scenario-geometry-and-convergence/19.3-SEED.md`).
E6 is the experiment where this was most visible: three configurations
(optimality 27-140) were published as `status="ok"` in 19.2 despite being
non-convergent (MF-07, and 19.2's anti-pattern #2). E6's scale axis
(`_scaled_depth_range`) also directly conflicted with the corrected
clearance bound as originally defined — `half_scale` could not clear it at
any board-to-surface standoff — which is why D-19.3-07 redefines the scale
axis to scale the depth extent *above* the derived floor rather than the
standoff below the surface. Phase 19.3 also runs the paired determinism
sweep (D-19.3-13/14) against this corrected geometry, comparing the cell
reproduction count to this pre-fix baseline of **63/308**.

**This is not (necessarily) an accuracy defect.** The evidence gathered
before the fix shows reconstruction accuracy is not distinguishable between
the high-optimality and low-optimality groups — `half_scale` (optimality
27/140) has the BEST accuracy in the table at 0.4935 mm. What was broken is
the optimality-as-convergence-diagnostic and (separately) the scale axis's
compatibility with any clearance bound at all. Do not read a moved E6 number
here as evidence the old numbers were wrong — only that the geometry and
axis definition generating the scenario are corrected.

## Why it is NOT under `experiments/results/`

`tests/unit/test_experiments_provenance.py` globs `experiments/results/` at
collection time and asserts every discovered artifact carries its provenance
record. Archived copies placed there would be swept into that gate as if
they were live results. This directory is deliberately outside that glob.

## How to use it

After the depth-clearance re-run, diff the regenerated
`generalization_sweep.csv` against the copy here. Report the optimality and
degenerate-guard-count change per configuration (D-19.3-11) — E6 has no
measured seed band (D-19.3-17), so do not claim accuracy changed either way
from this diff alone. Separately, compare the post-fix paired determinism
sweep's cell reproduction count against this baseline's 63/308 (D-19.3-14) —
report whatever the number is, whether or not it improves. Do not delete
this directory until MF-08 is written and the manuscript's numbers have been
reconciled.
