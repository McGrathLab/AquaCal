# E4 baseline — archived 2026-08-02, before the scenario depth-clearance fix

Byte-for-byte copies of E4's two manuscript-facing files, plus recorded
pointers to the nine per-cell provenance records, as they stood at commit
`c511429e3fcd6b4e052a1362f44965d1c12f7f33`, taken BEFORE Phase 19.3's
synthetic scenario depth-clearance fix.

## Why this exists

Every synthetic scenario generated by `generate_board_trajectory` /
`generate_real_rig_trajectory` allowed board corners to protrude through the
water surface. That put the residual on the refractive/pinhole hinge in
`compute_residuals` (the pinhole continuation guard added to fix the
`7e0cb90` absorbing-region defect), which made first-order optimality
unreadable as a convergence diagnostic for every affected experiment
(`.planning/phases/19.3-scenario-geometry-and-convergence/19.3-SEED.md`).
E4 is doubly affected: `GRID_DEPTH_RANGE` inherits the same shallow depth
range, and its cells are exactly where 19.2's anti-pattern #2 was caught
(cell 12x100 shipped 0.79 px RMS on optimality 6.4e9). Phase 19.3 derives a
real depth-clearance floor (D-19.3-01), re-centres board poses on the board
centre rather than a corner (D-19.3-19), and re-runs E4 against the
corrected geometry.

**This is not an accuracy defect.** The evidence gathered before the fix
shows reconstruction accuracy is not distinguishable between the
high-optimality and low-optimality groups; what was broken is the
optimality-as-convergence-diagnostic, not the calibration result. Do not
read a moved E4 number here as evidence the old numbers were wrong — only
that the geometry generating the scenario is corrected. Per D-19.3-17, E4
has no measured seed band, so its before/after reports the
optimality/degeneracy improvement WITHOUT an accuracy claim.

## Why it is NOT under `experiments/results/`

`tests/unit/test_experiments_provenance.py` globs `experiments/results/` at
collection time and asserts every discovered artifact carries its provenance
record. Archived copies placed there would be swept into that gate as if
they were live results. This directory is deliberately outside that glob.

## How to use it

After the depth-clearance re-run, diff the regenerated `benchmark_grid.csv`
against the copy here. Report the optimality and degenerate-guard-count
change per cell (D-19.3-11) — E4 has no measured seed band (D-19.3-17), so
do not claim accuracy changed either way from this diff alone. Do not delete
this directory until MF-08 is written and the manuscript's numbers have been
reconciled.
