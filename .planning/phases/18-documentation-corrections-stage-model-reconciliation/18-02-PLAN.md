Gate the DOCS-02 (BFS -> best-first) and DOCS-06 (three-stage model) renames on an explicit
user confirmation of the manuscript's vocabulary, and record the answer as the contract the
four rename plans implement against.

Purpose: `/gsd:discuss-phase` locked a three-stage vocabulary (D-04/D-05/D-06) and a

(`C:\Users\tucke\Desktop\main.pdf`) and found it says the OPPOSITE on both counts: a verbatim

abstract and Figure 2's own title. The evidence says that PDF is the stale pre-revision

recreate exactly the divergence this phase exists to close, and unwinding it after the keys

with it and are unaffected by the outcome either way.
