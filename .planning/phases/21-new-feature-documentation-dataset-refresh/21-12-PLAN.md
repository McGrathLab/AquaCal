<objective>
Settle whether the `n_water = 1.0` baseline can carry the paper's refractive-vs-non-refractive
claims — the cheap, decisive half of folded todo
`2026-08-05-verify-non-refractive-baseline-supports-paper-claims.md`.

Purpose: every `DegenerateObservationWarning` in the 19.4 production queue came from the
`n_water = 1.0` arm (14,949 and three others); the refractive arm reported zero. The guard states
that first-order optimality **and** reprojection RMS cannot be trusted to judge convergence for
that arm — and that arm is the comparison point for `main.tex` lines 68, 268, 270, 271, 278, 280,
281 and 295, including the abstract's headline. If the baseline is reported at a non-converged
solution, line 268's "refraction modeling is the sole experimental variable" is wrong and every
ratio becomes an upper bound of unknown tightness.

The todo records a source-reading argument that at `n_air = n_water = 1.0` the pinhole extension
**is** the refractive projection, so optimality is pessimistic rather than meaningless — and says
plainly that this "has not been verified numerically — verify before relying on it." That
verification is this plan.

**This plan is deliberately scheduled last, is independent of every other plan in the phase, and
is droppable.** If the SoftwareX deadline forces a scope cut, drop it whole; nothing depends on
it. It is the loosest fit of the three folded todos (CONTEXT.md "Folded Todos", note 3).
</objective>

**(1) The n=1 identity — todo step 1.** Construct a camera, an interface at a known `water_z`, and

them through the refractive projector and, separately, through the plain pinhole model. Assert

existing test file uses a stricter convention) for below-interface points, and note what happens
for above-interface points where the `h_q <= 0` continuation fires.

meaningless, and most of the todo's concern dissolves — the arms do differ only in refraction
modeling, and line 268 stands.

disagreement. Do not attempt to fix the projector — this phase does not touch `src/`.

Commit this as a permanent regression test in `tests/unit/test_refractive_geometry.py`, named so
its purpose is legible (e.g. `test_projection_reduces_to_pinhole_at_unit_index`), with a docstring
citing this plan and the manuscript lines that depend on it. Pinning the identity by test is what
stops it from silently regressing under a future optimizer change.

**(2) Classify the degenerate observations — todo step 2.** Determine whether any of the guard
