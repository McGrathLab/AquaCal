<objective>
Complete DATA-01b: move the large committed artifacts out of git now that they live in the
published archive, and restore the 1000 KB large-file guard repo-wide.

Purpose: the split is by function, not size — the `--check` baselines and offline CI smoke need
only ~20 KB of small CSVs, which stay versioned; nothing reads `calibration.json`
programmatically, and the external figures repo keeps its own copies of the residual and
reconstruction CSVs. The `exclude: ^experiments/results/` on `check-added-large-files` exists to
accommodate exactly these files, and removing them is what lets the guard come back.

**Sequencing is not optional:** this plan `depends_on` 21-09. The repository copies are the only
copies until the Zenodo version is published.

Output: three files removed from git, the pre-commit exclusion removed, the README's provenance
table repointed, and the one live consumer repaired.
</objective>
