<objective>
Make the published Newton convergence number describe the loop production actually runs, without
touching production numerics at all.

Purpose: EXP-07 and EXP-11, and the second of the two truths that failed phase verification. The
producing diagnostic `refractive_project_newton_diagnostic` states in its docstring that it "performs
the identical root-find as `_refractive_project_newton` — the shipped projection path used inside
every residual evaluation — via the shared `_solve_newton_r_p` helper, so the counts and residual
returned here describe production behavior rather than a re-implementation." That is **false**, and
was confirmed in source twice independently: `calibration/_optim_common.py:635` projects via
`refractive_project_batch` → `_refractive_project_newton_batch`, which contains its own hand-inlined
Newton loop, never calls `_solve_newton_r_p` (grep count 0), and terminates on
`np.all(np.abs(delta) < tolerance)`.

There are **three** Newton loops, not two. `_solve_newton_r_p`'s own docstring says it is "shared by
`_refractive_project_newton` and `refractive_project_newton_diagnostic`, so a change to the
convergence logic cannot drift between **two copies**." It says *two*. The batch loop is a third,
sitting outside the guarantee the helper was written to provide.

Output: an opt-in per-point diagnostic on the batch path, exported from `aquacal.core`; E3's tier 2
rewired to measure it; the false docstring corrected; and a bit-identity proof that production
projection did not move.

**Decision already taken (user, 2026-07-29): option (c) — instrument the batch path.** Options (a)
and (b) are recorded as rejected and must not be silently reopened. Migrating
`_refractive_project_newton_batch` onto `_solve_newton_r_p` is a desirable code-quality follow-up and
is **explicitly out of scope here**.

**Do not overstate the finding.** The computed positions are almost certainly correct. This is a
provenance defect, which is exactly what makes it citable and wrong.
</objective>
