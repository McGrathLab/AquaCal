<objective>
Write `experiments/e6_generalization_sweep.py`: three independent one-dimensional axes — refractive
index, layout, and scale — each passing through a common grid-family 12-camera baseline, emitting
tidy long-format rows that carry which axis varied.

Purpose: EXP-10 answers R1.4, which asked for physical experiments across multiple tanks, media,
and layouts. Physical multi-medium rigs are out of scope; controlled synthetic sweeps isolate each
variable more cleanly, and the single physical rig (E2) anchors them to reality.
Output: one script and fast unit tests. The production sweep is plan 19.2-11.

**Two constraints that are settled, not open.** (1) The baseline is
`build_grid_scenario(n_cameras=12, ...)` — NOT `create_scenario("realistic")`, which calls a
different generator (`generate_real_rig_array`, a fixed real-rig layout with no `layout`
parameter). A `realistic` baseline would sit off the layout axis entirely, and no axis value would
equal the baseline. (2) E5 and E6 are fully independent scripts; the "run them together" idea was
evaluated with numbers and dropped — their bands coincide at exactly one configuration, on
different geometries, so sharing saves under 5% of wall-clock in exchange for coupling two scripts
and a cache-key correctness risk. Do not re-raise either.
</objective>
