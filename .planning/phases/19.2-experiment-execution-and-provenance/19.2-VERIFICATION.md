### New Finding — three E6 configurations did not converge, published as `status="ok"`

Verified directly against the committed CSV (not from the SUMMARY):

| axis | axis_value | RMS px | optimality (interface) | optimality (intrinsic) | reconstruction_rmse_mm |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| index | 1.42 | 0.788808 | **51.924880** | 0.053633 | 0.570385 |
| layout | ring | 0.704959 | 0.001568 | **4.203286** | 0.717436 |
| scale | half_scale | 1.007156 | **27.319556** | **140.306722** | 0.493464 |
| (11 others) | | 0.70–0.79 | 0.0016–0.035 | 0.002–0.117 | 0.535–0.752 |

All fourteen rows read `status=ok`; nothing in the CSV distinguishes the three flagged rows from the
other eleven except the optimality columns themselves, which a reader has to know to look for and
interpret against a threshold that exists nowhere in the codebase (deliberately — D-12).

**Accuracy is independently confirmed unaffected**, re-derived directly from the CSV rather than
trusted from the SUMMARY: `layout=ring`'s reconstruction RMSE (0.717 mm) is the second-worst of all
fourteen rows, but the single worst (0.752 mm, `scale=double_scale`) belongs to a *healthy*
configuration (optimality 0.007/0.006); `scale=half_scale`, the single worst-converged row in the
table (optimality 140.3), has the *best* reconstruction accuracy of all fourteen rows (0.493 mm).
This directly corroborates `19.3-SEED.md`'s framing: a diagnostic failure (a max-norm gradient
inflated by a handful of pinhole-continued corners), not an accuracy failure.

  baseline, emitting tidy long-format rows carrying which axis varied") — SATISFIED as worded. It

  generator") — SATISFIED in the literal traceability sense checked above. The three flagged rows
  trace exactly as cleanly as the other eleven; what is missing is not a broken trace but a missing
  *caveat* a manuscript author would need before citing those rows.
- **Does escalating to Phase 19.3 make this acceptable for 19.2 to close?** Judged here as: **yes for
  the fix, no for the documentation.** The user's own recorded decision (`19.3-SEED.md`, 2026-08-01)
  to scope the scenario-geometry fix into its own phase, after 19.2 established a coherent baseline
