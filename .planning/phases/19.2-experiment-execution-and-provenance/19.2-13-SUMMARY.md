## Findings (Task 2)

All values from `experiments/results/index_sensitivity.csv`, sorted by `n_assumed`:

- **`scale_bias_pct`**: low end (`n_assumed=1.323`, `Δn=-0.010`) = **0.031125%**; Δn=0 control (`n_assumed=1.333`) = **0.041345%**; high end (`n_assumed=1.343`, `Δn=+0.010`) = **0.051970%**. The bias moves monotonically and materially across the band (~0.021 percentage points end to end).
- **`reprojection_rms_px`**: low end = **0.709459 px**; Δn=0 control = **0.706283 px**; high end = **0.707675 px**. The residual is essentially flat — the whole band spans roughly 0.706-0.709 px, with one point (`n_assumed=1.341`) at **0.803634 px**, a mild outlier relative to its neighbors (its Stage-3 solve reported a somewhat higher first-order optimality, 3.75e-03, than most other points' ~1e-3 range, suggesting a marginally less-tight convergence rather than a systematic trend — the two adjacent points, 1.339 and 1.343, are back in the 0.706-0.708 px range).
- **Maximum `bias_over_control`** (the within-band internal control, review M6): **0.010625** percentage points, at the high end of the band (`n_assumed=1.343`). This isolates the index effect against the band's own Δn=0 row — same geometry, same noise, same seed, same pipeline, differing only in the assumed index.
- **`holdout_floor_pct`** (live-read, D-13): **1.12345693%**, identical on every row. Verified by recomputation: `inter_corner_rmse_mm` (0.6740741599086391) from `experiments/results/real_rig_metrics.json` divided by the board's 60mm square size, times 100, equals 1.12345693... — matching the CSV exactly. Never hardcoded.
- **Maximum `scale_bias_over_floor`**: **0.046259**, at the high end of the band. Stated explicitly as the bound the WP4 argument uses, not a like-for-like comparison: it is the ratio of a signed bias measured on a synthetic 12-camera run at `noise_std=0.5 px` (E5) against E2's real-rig held-out RMSE (a different dataset, a different quantity, a different noise realization). The within-band control (`bias_over_control`, max 0.0106) is the comparison that actually isolates the index effect; `scale_bias_over_floor` situates that effect's magnitude relative to real-rig measurement noise.

**Confirmed the residual genuinely did not move as a null-result artifact, rather than assuming it.** `scale_bias_pct` varied end to end (automated verify: `abs(hi - lo) > 1e-9` passed, actual delta ≈0.0209 percentage points) while `reprojection_rms_px` stayed flat to two significant figures across the band's interior. This is the expected and meaningful outcome (bias moves, residual does not), not the doubly-flat signature of a threading defect. No STOP condition was triggered.

## `--check` Reproducibility Result

`python -m experiments.e5_index_sensitivity --check` exited **0**. Console output: `[index_sensitivity.csv] Fresh output matches committed baseline within tolerance.` This means `compare_experiment_csv` found every float cell within `CHECK_RTOL=1e-6` of the committed baseline and every non-float cell matched exactly, across all 17 `E5_COLUMNS` and all 11 rows.

**This is a genuine full-band reproduction, not a structurally-unknowable-column artifact.** The orchestrator flagged wave 5's E4 precedent (`_run_check` can never populate `exit_code`, so E4's `--check` exits 1 by design regardless of data quality) as a pattern to watch for. That precedent does not apply here: E5's `_run_check` reruns `run_band` end to end (the same code path `_run_full` uses) and compares the fresh DataFrame directly against the committed CSV with no columns that are structurally unpopulatable by a rerun — `scale_bias_pct_control` and `holdout_floor_pct` are both derived from the fresh band's own data (the Δn=0 row and the live-read JSON respectively), not carried over from the original run's process state. A passing `--check` here means the full pipeline — from `calibrate_synthetic` through `evaluate_calibration` through the control/floor post-processing — reproduced deterministically at `seed=42` across two independent ~22-minute runs.

`N_ASSUMED_BAND` in the committed source is byte-identical to its value before either run: `git diff experiments/e5_index_sensitivity.py` was empty both before the production launch and after `--check` completed (the only change to the file, `_run_check`'s implementation, was committed in `9ff27be` before the production run started).
