## Accomplishments

- `generate_board_trajectory` now centers its sampled working volume on the centroid of `camera_positions` by default (D-27), fixing a per-layout confound (cam0 -- a corner under `grid`, an end under `line` -- was previously the sampling center).
- D-27's non-inertness is contained mechanically, not asserted: two frozen-anchor exact-equality tests (`generate_real_rig_trajectory`, `create_scenario("realistic")`), a source-scanning grep-gate (no realistic-path caller ever passes `center`), plus four passing `--check` reproductions.
- The grid family (E4's cells, E6's baseline) moved to real-rig-like optical geometry: cameras at 1.031 m above the water surface, board depths in [1.1, 2.0] m, 0.43 m camera spacing -- the 0.72 x 0.54 m board now fits inside the camera frame at every depth in range (verified by computation, not literal).
- `xy_extent` now derives from a fixed fraction (0.54x) of the array's own XY footprint span rather than a fixed 0.15 m, so `grid`/`ring`/`line` each cover the identical fraction of their own footprint -- resolving E6's layout-axis confound together with D-27's centering fix.
- `run_grid_cell`'s held-out evaluation set is now built from the SAME geometry as its calibration trajectory (via a second `build_grid_scenario` call, mirroring E6's existing pattern), closing a silent bug where every cell's accuracy number would otherwise have been scored against a mismatched working volume after D-29.
- E6's `SCALE_AXIS_VALUES` rescaled around the new baseline, derived from E4's own constants rather than a second hardcoded copy of absolute numbers.

## Frozen-Anchor Capture Procedure (Task 1)

Anchors were captured at commit `7ae7ff640ea9ce561a540992d311a13556b4a190` (this plan's base, the merged result of wave 1) by:
1. Loading `aquacal.datasets.synthetic` from the working tree at that commit (before any edit in this plan).
2. Calling `generate_real_rig_trajectory(n_frames=100, depth_range=(1.1, 2.0), seed=42)` directly and recording every `rvec`/`tvec` component with Python's `repr()` (shortest round-trip float representation).
3. Calling `create_scenario("realistic", seed=42)` directly and recording its 30 board poses (`rvec`/`tvec`), 12 cameras' extrinsics (`R`, `t`), and `water_zs`, the same way.
4. Embedding the captured values as CSV-literal string constants in `tests/unit/test_datasets.py`, parsed at test time and compared via `np.testing.assert_array_equal` (exact, not `allclose`).

These anchors must never be regenerated to make a failing test pass -- a mismatch means D-27 leaked into a realistic-path caller.
