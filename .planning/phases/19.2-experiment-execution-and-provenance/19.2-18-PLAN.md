<objective>
Redesign the grid family's geometry: centre the calibration volume on the array it is meant to
calibrate (D-27), scale the working volume with the array footprint (D-28), and move the whole family
to real-rig optical geometry so the board fits in frame (D-29).

Purpose: EXP-08 and EXP-10. E4 and E6 exist to characterise cost and generalisation for deployments
like the real rig. Today the grid family sits **0.15 m** above the water with a 0.30 m footprint while
the real rig and the `realistic` preset both sit at **~1.03 m** with a ~1.34 m footprint, so the
0.72 × 0.54 m board — the same physical board in both cases — overfills the frame at a measured 22%
mean visibility. Worse, the calibration volume is pinned to a *corner* of the rig by an amount that
differs per layout (0.18 m for `grid`, 0.19 m for `ring`, **0.55 m** for `line`), which is the actual
cause of the `KeyError: 'cam11'` recorded in the committed `generalization_sweep.csv` and which makes
E6's layout axis confounded: a `ring` row reading slightly worse than `grid` may be measuring a
0.19 m centroid offset rather than anything about ring geometry.

**Nothing published moves.** E4 and E6 feed reviewer responses R1.4/R1.5/R3.2, not §3. E1, E5 and E7
already run at real-rig geometry through a *different* generator that this plan does not touch. See
`MANUSCRIPT-FINDINGS.md` MF-02.

**D-26 reconciliation — do not stall on this.** D-26 requires all `src` changes to land *and be proven
inert* before any experiment yielding a publishable result. D-27 is deliberately non-inert, so the
letter of D-26 cannot be met and an executor WILL hit this. The substance is preserved: every `src`
change still lands in waves 1–2 ahead of every production run, and the blanket inertness proof is
replaced by D-27's **containment gate** (tasks 1 and 3). Inertness is proven precisely where it must
hold — every path feeding a published number — and deliberately not claimed where the change is the
point. Treat that gate as D-26's requirement for this phase.

Output: a grid family whose scenes are representative, a containment gate that is mechanical, and an
E6 scale axis that still means what its labels say.

**Expect the 16×200 cell to fail in wave 3.** If corners per view rise from 19.5 toward 88, residuals
scale 3–4.5× and peak memory projects to 9.9–14.9 GiB against a ~15.7 GiB box. That is pre-authorised
and is wave 3's business, not this plan's. Do not pre-emptively shrink the geometry to make it fit —
choosing the geometry from the memory budget is choosing the range from the results.
</objective>
