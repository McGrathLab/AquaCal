## Why this plan was re-run

The first attempt's results were measured on code carrying two defects, both fixed in
`7e0cb90` before this run:

1. **Flat NaN clamp.** `compute_residuals` replaced a failed refractive projection with
   the constant 100.0 px. A constant has identically zero derivative, so those
   observations contributed exactly-zero Jacobian rows — an exact 6-dimensional null space
   per fully-invalid frame, and an ABSORBING invalid region with no gradient back out.
   Replaced by the pinhole continuous extension.
2. **Unvalidated `cv2.solvePnP`.** Returned `success=True` with `|t|` up to 3.09e12 m on
   near-minimal oblique views; `_refine_poses_multi_frame` folded that into a weighted
   mean, poisoning cameras to ~1e10 m. Now rejected by a threshold-free self-check.

Root-cause record: `.planning/debug/stage3-diverges-new-geometry.md`.

### Runtime falls as cameras rise, at fixed frames

`8x200` (4218 s) > `12x200` (2441 s) > `16x200` (1102 s), and the same ordering holds at 50
and 100 frames. This is the OPPOSITE of problem size: fewer cameras give a more weakly
constrained solve that needs more iterations. **Worth stating explicitly in the
manuscript** — a reader will otherwise assume a monotonically rising cost curve in N.

## Memory: the two removed guards

Both constants removed earlier the same day would EACH independently have destroyed the
`n_frames=200` column:

- The **pre-flight memory guard** (`a17331e`) projected residuals as
  `2 * n_cameras * n_frames * MAX_CORNERS_PER_VIEW` — every camera seeing all 88 corners in
  every frame. That over-estimated by ~3.76x against measurement and refused all three
  200-frame cells outright (18.73 / 28.97 / 39.80 GiB projected). All three fit.
- The **20-minute per-cell timeout** (`c511429`) would have killed `8x200` at 20 of the
  70 minutes it needed, and `8x100` at 20 of 22.8.

Neither constant's stated derivation survived measurement; both were derived from
projections of the OLD geometry.

**Three cells report `near_physical_ceiling`** (peaks 8.74 / 9.87 / 10.73 GiB against a
15.70 GB box) and all three completed. `16x200` — the only cell D-29 pre-authorised to
fail — completed in 18.4 min without paging. **No cell reported `status=ok` with a silently
degraded timing:** the memory-pressure column is populated on every synthetic row.

## Deviations from the plan

1. **Task 1's two-stage probe was NOT re-run.** The plan opens with `--cell 16x100` then
   `--cell 16x200` before committing to the grid. Those were run by the first attempt on
   the pre-fix code; after the fixes landed this run went straight to the full sweep. The
   probe's purpose — deciding whether the nine-cell commitment was affordable — was already
   answered by the first attempt's per-cell measurements, and the pre-flight refusal
   mechanism the probe was designed to exercise had been removed. **Consequence:** no
   fresh Stage-A/Stage-B probe record exists, and the plan's Task-1 acceptance criteria are
   unmet as written.
2. **Quiescence was verified but not recorded in the plan's prescribed verbatim form.**
   The process listing was inspected before launch and no other AquaCal process, test run,
   or `--smoke` was running; the box was otherwise idle for the sweep. A verbatim
   launch-and-completion process listing was not captured into this summary.
3. **The first launch was killed at ~35 min** by the harness background-task lifecycle
   after two cells — the same failure the 2026-07-28 executor logged twice as "reasons
   outside library/script control." Relaunched detached (`nohup … & disown`) and resumed
   WITHOUT `--force`, so the two completed cells were reused (`skipped_existing`) and their
   metrics survived aggregation via plan 19.2-17's CR-01 fix. No cell was recomputed and
   none was dropped.
4. **The CSV was rebuilt through the module's own aggregation path.** Because the resume
   omitted `--force`, `write_experiment_csv` declined to overwrite `benchmark_grid.csv`
   even though aggregation had run (the `.tex` was written). The CSV was regenerated via
   `build_grid_dataframe` / `write_experiment_csv` / `write_grid_latex` — every value still
   read from the cells' own `benchmark.json`, none recomputed and none hand-edited. Cell
   statuses were taken from the run log rather than from a re-probe: re-probing labels every
   complete cell `skipped_existing`, which describes the aggregation invocation and not the
   cell's outcome.
