<objective>
Close CR-02 and WR-08 in E6's resume path, and land D-31's E6 half: the per-configuration
checkpoints become real provenance records and the sweep gains an environment sidecar.

Purpose: EXP-10 and EXP-11. Two of this phase's seven must-have truths failed verification; the
first was that E5 and E6 carry a seed and nothing else. E6's half is closed here rather than in a
later wave because the checkpoint write site and the resume read site are **the same three lines of
code**, and touching that file twice for one bug and one omission is how the reason string got
erased in the first place.

Landing this in wave 1 (rather than wave 2, where GAP-CONTEXT's map lists D-31) is deliberate and
satisfies the constraint that matters: the scripts must already emit provenance before waves 3–5
run, or every artifact needs regenerating twice. Wave 1 is earlier, not later.

record; tests that fail on the base commit.

`LAYOUT_AXIS_VALUES`, or `SCALE_AXIS_VALUES` here — the scale axis is rescaled in wave 2 by plan
19.2-18, which owns the grid-family geometry. Do NOT add a pass/fail verdict column (D-12 stands).
