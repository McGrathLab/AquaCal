<objective>
Close the two instrumentation gaps E4's grid collides with: `SolverDiagnostics` gains
`n_residuals` (so D-15's Jacobian element count is a measured library-side quantity, not a
script-side re-derivation of the `min_corners` filter), and
`experiments/_io.py::write_direct_call_benchmark` gains an optional `memory_readings` parameter
(D-24) so D-06's `memory_out` readings can actually reach `benchmark.json`, plus an optional `seed`
parameter that stamps the run's seed into `solver_config` (review H5).

Purpose: without the first, E4 cannot report "n_residuals x n_params" without re-implementing
library filtering logic — the exact P2 violation E3 tier 3 exists to prevent. Without the second,
the wrapper discards memory readings at `_io.py:427` and E4's synthetic cells would carry no
`memory` block at all.
Without the third, **no benchmark record in the repository carries a seed at all** — verified
against `experiments/results/e1_benchmark_refractive.json`, whose `solver_config` holds
`ftol`/`gtol`/`xtol`/`loss_scale`/`n_air`/`n_water`/`refine_intrinsics`/`robust_loss`/
`shared_interface` and nothing else — while EXP-11 and ROADMAP SC5 both require every committed
result to carry its seed.
Output: one additive diagnostics field wired at three call sites, two additive wrapper parameters,
and tests proving all three defaults preserve every committed record's shape.
</objective>
