## E5 — the science moved, and the movement is fully explained. But the gate as written could not be evaluated.

### The attribution gate is unrunnable as specified

Plan 23 requires the mechanism be *"confirmed from `discard_stats.pnp_guard_rejected > 0` in the
run's own output before any row is accepted."* **E5 emits no `discard_stats`.** Searched
`e5_band.log` and every JSON under `experiments/results/` — no hits.

This is the same defect class as plan 06's D-34 gate, which was unrunnable until plan 19.2-26
added the counters: **a gate that names evidence the experiment does not produce.** Plan 26
instrumented the calibration path; E5's own entry points were not covered. Recorded as a finding
rather than worked around.

Attribution below therefore rests on magnitude-against-noise plus an exact source trace — not on
the guard counter the plan asked for. **That is weaker than the plan intended**, and the gap is
stated rather than papered over.

Its seed band is **exactly zero**: identical across all five seeds. A quantity invariant to seed
that moves is definitionally not seed noise, and under plan 23 that is a halt candidate.

It is not a halt. `load_holdout_floor_pct` reads `inter_corner_rmse_mm` **live out of E2's
committed `real_rig_metrics.json`** — D-13 requires reading it live and forbids hardcoding it —
and **E2 was re-run last night under D-34** (`faa05b3`). E5's yardstick moved because its source
moved, exactly as designed:

   calibration path or a gate rewritten around evidence that exists.

   help text). Windows-only, cosmetic; the queue works around it with `PYTHONIOENCODING=utf-8`.
