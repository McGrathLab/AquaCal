## Evidence captured for phase 19.3

Plan 29's amended criteria required recording degenerate-observation counts, since
`generate_real_rig_trajectory` shares the `depth_range = (1.1, 2.0)` default implicated in E6.

**E5 IS affected, but far less severely than E6:**

| | E5 | E6 |
|---|---|---|
| configurations emitting `DegenerateObservationWarning` | 4 of 11 | 14 of 14 |
| degenerate observations, per occurrence | **3** (all four) | 2–38 |
| optimality on affected points | 7.8e-4, 8.7e-4, 1.4e-3, 1.3e-2 | up to 51.9 |
| reproduces bit-identically | **yes** | no (63/308 cells moved) |

Two findings worth carrying into 19.3:

1. **The blast radius is confirmed at five experiments** — E5's real-rig geometry is affected, so the
   defect is not confined to the grid family. An earlier read of this run's first clean points
   suggested otherwise and was wrong.
2. **A few kinks do not unpin the solution.** E5 hit 3 degenerate observations on four points and
   still reproduced exactly, while E6's 19- and 38-observation configurations did not reproduce. So
   "any degeneracy ⇒ non-determinism" is false; it takes enough of them. This matches the weak
   correlation measured in plan 28 (corr = 0.19) and should shape how 19.3 tests the link.
3. **In E5 the degeneracy appears only as `n_assumed` departs from `n_true`** — the six points
   nearest the true index were clean. That suggests index misspecification distorts the estimated
   geometry until corners cross the interface, which is a *different* mechanism from E6's
   too-shallow trajectory, and 19.3 should not assume one fix addresses both.
