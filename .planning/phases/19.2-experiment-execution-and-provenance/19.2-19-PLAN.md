<objective>
Give E5 the three provenance fields it never captured, and stop two cwd-dependent behaviours from
corrupting its re-run.

Purpose: EXP-09, EXP-11, and the first of the two truths that failed phase verification. Neither
`experiments/e5_index_sensitivity.py` nor `experiments/e6_generalization_sweep.py` imports
`capture_environment`, `assemble_benchmark_record`, or `write_direct_call_benchmark` — grep returns
zero hits in both, against 4 for E3 and 6 for E4. There is therefore no AquaCal version, no git SHA,
and no Python/NumPy/SciPy/OS environment recorded anywhere for two of the four experiments this
phase produced. E6's half is closed by plan 19.2-16; this plan closes E5's.

Output: `experiments/e5_index_sensitivity.py` writing an E3-shaped `e5_provenance.json`, with the
noise-floor path anchored and `--check` guarded.

**The CSV header must not change.** WR-04 offers two fixes — new columns, or an E3-style sidecar
carrying the run configuration — and this plan takes the sidecar. The reason is specific: E5 is
re-run in wave 5 **for provenance only**. Its numbers are deterministic and seeded and its `--check`
exits 0 today, so a moved science column in that re-run is a **determinism defect and a hard stop**,
not expected churn. That check is only meaningful if the header is stable, and adding columns here
would make wave 5's comparison a header mismatch instead of a determinism proof.
</objective>
