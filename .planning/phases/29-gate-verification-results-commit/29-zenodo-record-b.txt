--- 1.2  `isNewVersionOf` is EXCLUDED under every option, and why --------------

Under no option does either record assert the version relation (the `related_identifiers`
relation naming a previous version) against record 21889922.

Reason: Zenodo's versioning is a FIRST-CLASS mechanism — a `newversion` deposition action
that produces a new version inside the SAME concept record, sharing a concept DOI. Records
A and B are NEW concept records, not new versions of 21889922. Asserting a version
relationship in `related_identifiers` against a different concept record would tell a
reader, falsely, that following 21889922's concept DOI reaches these records. It does not
and never will.

What is asserted instead:
  - `references` -> 10.5281/zenodo.21889922, which is true: these records reference that
    one without claiming to be a version of it; and
  - an explicit supersession sentence in prose, in BOTH Record B's Zenodo description and
    the README inside its zip, so a reviewer who unzips the 4.35 GB historical bundle is
    told plainly that its `reference_outputs/` are superseded.

This is required by the folded todo (`2026-08-15-repackage-and-reupload-the-zenodo-archive.md`
§ Do not: "Do not silently reuse the existing version DOI", "Do not delete the old
version") and by threat register rows T-29-46 and T-29-49.

Record 21889922 is not modified, deleted, re-versioned or withdrawn by this plan.
