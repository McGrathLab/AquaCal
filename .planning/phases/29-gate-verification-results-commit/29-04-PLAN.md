<objective>
Build Record A — the immutable input package — and stream it into an unpublished Zenodo draft on
production.

Purpose: D-29-04 makes this the phase's critical path. At roughly 4 GB the transfer is hours, the
paper submits within days, and Record A holds *inputs*, which no grading result can invalidate. It
is therefore started as early as D-29-06's rehearsal gate allows and runs **in parallel with all
grading work** — this plan and plan 29-03 are the same wave for exactly that reason.

revision of a few megabytes of results costs a full 4.35 GB re-upload. Splitting it is the whole
point of RUN-05.

Output: a built input archive with a recorded md5, and an unpublished production draft whose id,
web URL and bucket round-trip proof are in the repository.
</objective>
