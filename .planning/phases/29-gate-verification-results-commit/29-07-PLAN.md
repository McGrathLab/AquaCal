<objective>
Build Record B — the versioned results package — and stage it as an unpublished Zenodo draft, now
that grading has passed.

Purpose: D-29-05 puts this strictly after RUN-03's gate verification and the E2 same-seed control,
so the results package can never contain numbers a later check disowns. It costs nothing on the
critical path, because Record A is the long pole and its transfer already ran in parallel.

Record B is a few megabytes and is re-versioned whenever results change — which is the entire point
of the split. Record `21889922` bundles inputs and outputs into one 4.35 GB zip, so Zenodo's
new-version flow has no file to carry forward and every future results revision costs a full
re-upload. After the split, Record A becomes genuinely immutable and Record B becomes the
self-contained thing a reviewer wants to inspect: config, outputs and run manifest together.

Output: Record B's payload, its metadata, and an unpublished production draft with a verified
round trip.
</objective>

asserting a version relationship in `related_identifiers` against a **different concept record**
