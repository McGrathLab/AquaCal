# Phase 19.4 Plan 03: Pre-run reviewer-intent coverage matrix Summary

One-liner: built and got human approval on a 12-row reviewer-point -> work-package -> experiment
coverage matrix that gates the 9.5-hour production queue; verdict is CONFIRMED with two PARTIAL
rows, one declined-by-design (R1.2) and one an already-accepted tradeoff (R1.4/E6) re-surfaced
rather than newly discovered.

## What was built

Task 1 read the OneDrive manuscript tree (read-only: `reviewer_response_plan.md`,
`reviewer_responses.md`, `main.tex`) plus `.planning/MANUSCRIPT-FINDINGS.md` (MF-05, MF-08, MF-09)
and `19.4-CONTEXT.md`'s D-19.4-13/16/17, and produced
`.planning/phases/19.4-grid-family-clearance-floor-fix/19.4-COVERAGE-MATRIX.md`: a single table
mapping all 12 reviewer points (R1.1-R1.5, R2, R3.1-R3.3, R4.1-R4.3) from
`reviewer_response_plan.md`'s master triage table to their work package, assigned experiment (of
E1/E3/E4/E5/E6/E7), the artifact that experiment produces after this phase's fix, whether it
carries an accuracy claim under D-19.3-17, and a YES/PARTIAL/NO verdict on whether the experiment
as it will actually run still answers the question.

Result: **10/12 YES, 2/12 PARTIAL**. R1.2 (analytic Jacobian derivation) is declined by design —
an advisor-approved scope decision predating this audit, not a queue gap. R1.4 (physical
generalization across tanks/media/layouts) is the sharp case D-19.4-17 was written to force into
the open: E6 answers with movement across index/layout/scale but carries no accuracy claim (no
seed band under D-19.3-17), while WP5's own draft response text says the reply should "report
accuracy holds across the sweep" — a claim E6 as scoped cannot honestly support.

Task 2 presented the matrix at a blocking human checkpoint (Task type
`checkpoint:human-verify`, `gate="blocking"`) and did not self-approve. The user approved the
CONFIRMED verdict as written, after flagging one arithmetic error in the matrix's own prose (the
Verdict section said "eleven of twelve...YES" against a table that has 10 YES / 2 PARTIAL). That
was corrected and committed (`999fbc2`) before recording the approval.
