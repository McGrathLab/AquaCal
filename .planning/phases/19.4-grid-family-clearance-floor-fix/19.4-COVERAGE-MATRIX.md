## Remedy paragraphs for PARTIAL/NO rows

### Row 2 — R1.2, PARTIAL

The full analytic-Jacobian derivation and accuracy/cost head-to-head is **declined by design**,
not by a gap this queue can close. The master triage table records it as `Decline (justify)` and
the advisor decisions section confirms: *"R1.2 analytic Jacobian: DECLINE the full derivation;
justify FD + quantify cost (WP3 item 5 + WP2)."* E4's benchmark grid (132.5 min, already in the
queue at position 2) answers the cost half completely; the accuracy half is answered by argument,
not measurement — FD accuracy is not the question at issue once CPR grouping collapses evaluation
count to a near-constant ~13–17, and no experiment in this suite or any addition to it would
change that rebuttal. **No queue amendment.** Cost of doing it anyway: not costed, out of scope
for a tools paper per the response plan's own reasoning.

### Row 4 — R1.4, PARTIAL — the sharp case this audit exists to name

This is the row D-19.4-17 was written to force into the open. WP5's own text says the response
should **"report accuracy holds across the sweep"** — but under D-19.3-17 applied strictly, E6
carries **no accuracy claim**: it has no seed band, so "accuracy holds" cannot honestly be written
against its output as this queue currently produces it. As it will run after this phase, E6
answers R1.4 with *movement across index/layout/scale, and nothing more* — an honest non-answer to
the literal ask, not a false one.

**This is not a newly discovered gap; it is a previously accepted one, now re-surfaced.**
D-19.4-13 explicitly deferred an E6 band with its cost stated up front: *"E4 and E6 bands are
deliberately NOT in this phase — E6 ×10 is 16.6 h and E4 ×10 is 22.1 h, ≈39 h together. Neither
carries an accuracy claim, so neither needs a band for the manuscript. Recorded in Deferred Ideas
with its cost."* That is a user decision made with full knowledge of exactly this consequence.

**The concrete remedy, costed:** reinstating a 10-seed E6 band (matching E7 and E1's `--seeds`
treatment under D-19.4-14) costs **~16.6 h** at ~100 min/seed — nearly double the entire 9 h 30 min
queue this phase already commits to, against an Aug 21 hard deadline 17 days out. A partial band
(e.g. 3 seeds) is **not** an option: MF-08 states explicitly that "n = 3 is not a band and no band
may be quoted from it," so a partial reinstatement would not upgrade the verdict from PARTIAL to
YES — it would only cost hours for no honesty gain.

**Recommendation carried into the verdict below:** accept PARTIAL for this phase, on the strength
of the fact that it was already knowingly accepted once by the user in D-19.4-13, and word the R1.4
response prose honestly — report the sweep's movement across index/layout/scale as *demonstrated
generalization behavior*, explicitly without an accuracy claim, rather than overstating it. The
16.6 h band remains available as a follow-on run, off the critical path, any time before the Aug 21
submission if the author decides the honest non-answer is not acceptable for the response letter.
