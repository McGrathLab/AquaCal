# Plan 19.4-07 Summary — Fail-fast in E4 and E6

## What was built

D-19.4-11 wired into both record-and-continue sinks in `experiments/`, with
`--no-fail-fast` as the explicit opt-out on each.

Before this plan, both scripts wrapped their per-unit work in a blanket
`except Exception`, recorded the failure as a `status="failed"` row, and
**exited 0**. That behaviour cost 74 and 84 minutes at seeds 47 and 50 while
reporting success. Plan 19.4-09 runs a 9 h 30 min unattended queue; a `src`
defect there must stop the run rather than fill a CSV with failed rows and
report success at the end.

- **E4 (`e4_benchmark_grid.py`)** — two layers: the child subprocess re-raises,
  and `_run_full`'s cell loop stops on the first non-ok / non-`skipped_existing`
  status.
- **E6 (`e6_generalization_sweep.py`)** — single layer: `run_sweep` raises
  `FailFastAbort` for the first configuration whose outcome records
  `status="failed"`, covering the fresh-run and cached-checkpoint paths
  together. `run_configuration`'s own `except` block is deliberately left
  untouched.

The abort message names the configuration key, the exception, and — for a
clearance rejection — both the supplied minimum and the derived floor.
