<objective>
Wire fail-fast into E4 and E6 so an unattended overnight run aborts on the first failed cell or
configuration and exits non-zero, with `--no-fail-fast` as the explicit opt-out (D-19.4-11, SC-6).

Purpose: today both scripts wrap their per-unit work in a blanket `except Exception` that records
the failure as a `status="failed"` row and continues, exiting 0. That cost 74 and 84 minutes at
seeds 47 and 50 while reporting success. This phase's queue runs 9 h 30 min unattended; a src
defect must stop it, not fill a CSV with failed rows.

Output: fail-fast in both scripts, a `--no-fail-fast` flag on both, and tests asserting exit code
and message content.

**Do NOT widen this to E1, E5 or E7.** Grep confirms E4 and E6 are the only two files in
`experiments/` with a record-and-continue `except Exception` sink. E1/E5/E7 do not loop over
independent configurations; an exception there already propagates unhandled, which is fail-fast
by omission. Adding machinery to them is inventing scope.

**Do NOT touch `board_clearance_floor` or `margin_factor`** — out of scope, and tuning `k` to make
a failing case pass is blocking anti-pattern 5.

**This plan MUST NOT run E4 or E6 at production scale.** A full E4 is 132.5 min; a full E6 is
~100 min. Verification uses a deliberately-failing configuration under a scratch `--out` and, at
most, E6 `--smoke`. Production runs belong to the queue in plan 09.
</objective>
