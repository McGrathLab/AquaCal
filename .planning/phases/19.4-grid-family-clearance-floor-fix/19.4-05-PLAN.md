<objective>
Add a shared seed-band mechanism to `experiments/_io.py` and wire a `--seeds` mode into E7 that
emits a committed `interface_ablation_band.csv` (D-19.4-14, SC-5a).

Purpose: E7's per-arm bands are MF-05's evidence and the milestone's ONLY surviving accuracy
claim (D-19.3-17 demoted E1 and E5). Today those bands are not reproducible — 19.3 produced them
with ad-hoc harnesses writing to `seed_sweep_19_3/`, which is gitignored. What survives is a
summary table a reviewer must take on trust. That contradicts the standard this phase is held to.

Output: `parse_seed_list` and `run_seed_band` in `experiments/_io.py`, a `--seeds` flag on E7
producing a 480-row band CSV (10 seeds x 48 rows = 12 cameras x 4 arms in the `"realistic"`
production scenario), and the resolved seed list recorded in E7's provenance sidecars.

**E7 is INERT under this phase's interface fix** (CONTEXT.md § CORRECTION). Its `--seeds` band is
justified by reproducibility, not by movement. Its single-seed `interface_ablation.csv` must
byte-match after the fix — that is plan 04's gate, not this plan's concern, but do not write
anything here that could perturb the `--seed 42` default path.

**E4 and E6 do NOT get `--seeds` in this phase** — their bands cost ~39 h and neither carries an
accuracy claim. Write the mechanism so adding them later is configuration, not new code.

**This plan MUST NOT run the 10-seed band.** A full E7 band is ~56 min and belongs to the
production queue (plan 09). Verify with a 2-seed band under a scratch `--out` directory only.
</objective>

    row-wise with `ignore_index=True`. No exception handling: a failing seed propagates, matching
    D-19.4-11's fail-fast posture rather than recording a partial band.
