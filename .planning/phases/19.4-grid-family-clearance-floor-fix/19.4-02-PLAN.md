<objective>
Move `generate_camera_array`'s `height_variation` jitter from `water_z` (the world-frame Z of the
water *surface*) onto `C_z` (camera height), so every synthetic scenario models one flat water
surface shared by all cameras — and add the scenario-invariant test whose absence is why this
shipped.

Purpose: the ground truth currently gives each camera its own water plane, contradicting the
single-flat-interface premise the method and the manuscript rest on. `Interface`'s own docstring
(`src/aquacal/core/interface_model.py:8-22`) says the value is "the Z-coordinate of the water
surface in world frame" and that "after optimization this is the same value (water_z) for all
cameras"; the parameter is documented as "Std dev of per-camera **height** variation". The intent
was camera height; the implementation moved the interface.

Output: the corrected generator, a new `tests/unit/test_synthetic_scenario_geometry.py`, and a
green unfiltered suite.

**Do NOT set `height_variation = 0`.** That would remove the `C_z <-> water_z` degeneracy that E7
exists to probe. D-19.4-09 is explicit and locked: relocate the jitter, do not delete it.
Warning sign that this went wrong: E7's `percamera_fixed` arm shows no height/distance scatter at
all afterwards.
</objective>
