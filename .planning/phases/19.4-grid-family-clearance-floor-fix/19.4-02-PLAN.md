**The one asymmetry that IS the bug.** `default_xy_extent_for_layout` may re-derive internally
with `seed=0` because camera XY positions are seed-invariant — its own docstring says so.
`max(water_zs)` is seed-DEPENDENT; it is exactly the quantity that moves. The new helper **must
take the caller's already-built `water_zs` as a parameter and must never build a camera array of
its own.** A helper taking `(n_cameras, layout, spacing, seed)` and rebuilding internally would
reproduce this defect in a new location (D-19.4-07).

**`depth_range=None` is FORBIDDEN as the remedy** (D-19.4-02). It is bit-identical for the
calibration scene and shifts the HOLDOUT scene by up to 0.469 mm — measured, not predicted. The
guard's own error message recommends it; the guard is wrong about this.

**The anchor test from plan 01 is the gate.** If it fails, the fix moves — never the tolerance,
never the anchor (anti-pattern 3, blocking).
