# Phase 19.4 — Re-scope proposal

**Drafted:** 2026-08-04
**Status:** DRAFT for user review. Nothing applied. The seven existing plans and
`19.4-CONTEXT.md` still describe the *pre-re-scope* phase.
**Trigger:** the user's observation that the paper models a single flat interface, so the
ground truth should never carry a per-camera water surface.

---

## What changed

`generate_camera_array` (`synthetic.py:259`) applies the per-camera jitter to **`water_z` —
the world-frame Z of the water surface** — while leaving every camera at `C_z = 0`. The
`Interface` docstring is explicit that this value is *"the Z-coordinate of the water surface in
world frame"* and that *"after optimization this is the same value (water_z) for all cameras."*

So the ground truth has **one water surface per camera**. A single board corner is refracted
through a different plane by each camera. That is not a flat interface; it is not a surface.

It stayed invisible because every camera sits at `C_z = 0`, and `h_c = water_z − C_z` makes
"jitter the plane" and "jitter the camera height" numerically identical. The parameter is
documented as *"Std dev of per-camera **height** variation"* — the intent was camera height and
the implementation moved the interface.

### Audited: three of five scenario sources are wrong

| source | cams | distinct `water_z` | spread | verdict | used by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `create_scenario("ideal")` | 4 | 1 | 0.00 mm | OK | E1 |
| `create_scenario("realistic")` | 12 | 1 | 0.00 mm | OK | — |
| `generate_real_rig_array()` | 12 | 1 | 0.00 mm | OK | E3, E5 |
| `create_scenario("minimal")` | 2 | **2** | 3.12 mm | **BAD** | **E7** |
| `generate_camera_array` (σ=5 mm) | 12 | **12** | 12.15 mm | **BAD** | **E4, E6** |

E2 is real-rig data — unaffected.

### Measured: the error exceeds the residuals being reported

Same 30-frame board trajectory, seed 42, projected through both geometries — twelve planes as
shipped versus one plane with the jitter moved to `C_z`. 31,680 corner observations:

| statistic | value |
|---|---|
| mean displacement | **1.42 px** |
| median | 1.28 px |
| p95 | 3.46 px |
| **max** | **6.33 px** |

E4/E6 reprojection RMS is **~0.4–0.9 px**. The modelling error is larger than the residual it
is being measured against, and it scales monotonically with each camera's jitter — `cam0`
(jitter 0.000 mm) moves exactly 0.0000 px; `cam3` (−6.511 mm) moves 6.33 px.

**This is a dominant systematic, not a rounding detail.**

## Proposed new phase definition

**Name:** Single Flat Interface *(was: Grid-Family Clearance Floor Fix)*
**Directory:** keep `19.4-grid-family-clearance-floor-fix/` — renaming costs git history for no
benefit; the ROADMAP title carries the real name.

**Goal:** Every synthetic scenario models **one flat water surface shared by all cameras**,
matching the physical premise the method and the manuscript rest on — with the per-camera
*interface distance* variation preserved by moving it onto camera height, where it belongs. The
three affected experiments are re-measured on the corrected geometry.

**Depends on:** Phase 19.3.

### The fix

```python
# generate_camera_array, replacing the water_z jitter at :259
delta       = 0.0 if i == 0 else rng.normal(0, height_variation)
C_z         = -delta                    # camera height carries the variation
water_z     = height_above_water        # ONE plane, shared by every camera
```

`h_c = water_z − C_z` is preserved exactly per camera (verified on all 12 at seed 42), so the
*intent* — cameras at slightly different distances from the surface — survives intact. What
changes is that they now look through the **same** surface.

### Success criteria

1. **Every synthetic scenario source yields exactly one distinct `water_z`**, asserted by a test
   that enumerates all three `create_scenario` presets, `generate_real_rig_array`, and
   `generate_camera_array` across layouts and seeds. Its absence is why this shipped.
2. The jitter moves to `C_z` with `h_c` preserved per camera, proven by exact comparison.
3. **E1, E3 and E5 are proven bit-inert** — `ideal` uses `height_variation=0.0`, so the
   corrected code reduces to `C_z = 0`, `water_z = height_above_water`, identical to today; E3
   and E5 never call `generate_camera_array`. Proof, not assumption.
4. `GRID_DEPTH_RANGE` re-derived from the corrected geometry. With `max(water_zs) =
   height_above_water` at every seed, the clearance floor is **seed-invariant by construction**.
5. **E4, E6 and E7 re-measured**, with movement reported against each experiment's own measured
   seed band where one exists, and with **no accuracy claim** where none exists (D-19.3-17).
6. Fail-fast on the first failed cell, exiting non-zero (carried over unchanged from D-19.4-06).

### What the re-scope does to the old plans

| old plan | fate |
|---|---|
| 19.4-01 pose anchor capture | **keep, re-purposed** — anchors now prove E1/E3/E5 inert rather than E4/E6 |
| 19.4-02 E4 helper + rename | **shrink** — the floor is seed-invariant now, so this is re-deriving a constant, not building a per-scenario mechanism |

**The single biggest simplification:** D-19.4-02's whole reason for existing was preserving
bit-inertness at seed 42. Once the interface correction moves the numbers deliberately, there is
nothing to preserve, and `max(GRID_DEPTH_RANGE[0], derived)` collapses to just deriving the
floor. D-19.4-03's checkpoint-identity gymnastics likewise become unnecessary.

## Open decisions — these need the user

- **R-01: does E7's 10-seed band get re-measured, or is seed 42 enough?**
  E7 is the experiment this most affects and MF-05 is a headline finding. 19.3 established that
  a band measured on *superseded* geometry cannot support a claim — that is exactly why E1 was
  demoted. **Re-measuring the band is therefore required if E7 is to keep its accuracy claim**,
  and E7 is currently the *only* experiment that has one. Cost: 10× a single E7 run; that
  per-run cost is **not known to me and must be measured before committing**.
  *Recommendation: re-measure. Otherwise E7 loses its claim and the milestone has none.*

- **R-02: archive before change?** The 19.3 precedent (plan 03) archived pre-fix artifacts under
  `experiments/archive/{exp}-{date}-{reason}/` with a provenance README. Seven such archives
  exist. *Recommendation: yes, `e{4,6,7}-2026-08-XX-pre-interface-fix/`.*

- **R-03: keep per-scenario derivation, or accept the constant?** The floor is genuinely
  seed-invariant after the fix, so a module constant is *correct*. Deriving per-scenario is
  still better hygiene and costs nothing, but it is no longer the phase's thesis.
  *Recommendation: derive, demoted to hygiene.*

- **R-04: schedule.** 17 days to 2026-08-21, with Phase 21's dataset regeneration still ahead
  and its own publication blocker (DATA-01a). This phase now carries real compute.
  *No recommendation — this is a judgement about the deadline, not about the code.*

## Manuscript consequences (recorded, not actioned — the user owns the manuscript)

- **MF-05** (E7 shared-vs-per-camera) — its band is measured on geometry this phase supersedes.
- **MF-08** — its E4/E6/E7 rows move again.
- **A new finding is warranted**: synthetic validation for E4/E6/E7 was performed against a
  ground truth with per-camera water surfaces, contradicting the single-flat-interface premise;
  found and corrected pre-resubmission. Worth stating plainly — it is the kind of thing a
  reviewer who reads the generator would otherwise find first.
- The correction **strengthens** E7's published conclusion rather than weakening it: shared mode
  won 10/10 against a ground truth biased toward per-camera. On corrected geometry shared is the
  *correct* model, so the result should hold at least as strongly.
