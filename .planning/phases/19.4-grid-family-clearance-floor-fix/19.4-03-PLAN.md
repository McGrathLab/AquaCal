<objective>
Produce and get approval for a pre-run coverage matrix mapping every reviewer point to the
experiment assigned to answer it, and a verdict on whether that experiment — as it will run after
this phase — actually answers the question (D-19.4-17, SC-8).

Purpose: these experiments were designed months ago to answer specific reviewer points. Since
then the suite has been through phases 19.1, 19.2, 19.3 and now 19.4. E3 was added after
planning; E5 and E1 lost their accuracy claims to D-19.3-17; four of six experiments now report
movement with no accuracy claim at all. Whether that still answers the reviewer who asked the
question is open and unexamined. Finding a gap now is cheap. Finding it after a 9 h 30 min
overnight run, or after resubmission, is not.

Output: `19.4-COVERAGE-MATRIX.md` plus an approved verdict (CONFIRMED or AMENDED) that plan 08
encodes into the queue's stage list.

**This is an audit of intent, not of code. It cannot be satisfied by checking that experiments
exit 0.** "Did it run" is not the question; "does the output answer what was asked" is.

**This plan GATES the queue.** Plan 08 (queue script) and plan 09 (queue execution) both depend
on it. No production stage launches until this matrix is reviewed and its verdict recorded.
</objective>
