- Placed `derive_grid_depth_range` immediately before its only call site (the `GRID_DEPTH_RANGE` assignment) rather than literally beside `default_xy_extent_for_layout`, since the helper is referenced at import time and Python requires the definition to precede that reference. `default_xy_extent_for_layout`'s docstring now names `derive_grid_depth_range` as its intended sibling, satisfying D-19.4-12's placement intent without a forward reference. Replaced with "~5.6 mm drop from the pre-fix constant".
- Confirmed via direct read of `synthetic.py` (`create_scenario`'s `"realistic"` branch, line 1141-1142) that the CORRECTED rationale in `19.4-CONTEXT.md` § CORRECTION is accurate: `generate_real_rig_array()` assigns the frozen module-level `WATER_Z` unconditionally, with no seed dependence and no `generate_camera_array` call anywhere in that path.
