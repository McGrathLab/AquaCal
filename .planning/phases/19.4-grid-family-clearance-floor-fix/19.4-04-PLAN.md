<objective>
Assert legality by construction, exhaustively and cheaply, as a permanent test in the suite. The
absence of this test is why the defect shipped.

Purpose: the frozen constant existed because someone derived it once, at one seed, and
generalised — anti-pattern 7, blocking, and the anti-pattern that produced this phase. The
structural answer is not a better single-seed check; it is asserting the property across 13,500
scenarios in ~8 seconds. Research measured 0.593 ms per scenario at production `n_frames=100`
(0.107 s for a 180-scenario reduced run), so the full sweep needs no `slow` marker.

Output: two tests in `tests/unit/test_experiments_e4.py` — the exhaustive sweep, and a targeted
regression for the four seeds whose failure modes the diagnosis characterised.
