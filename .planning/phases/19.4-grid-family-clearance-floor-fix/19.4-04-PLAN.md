<objective>
Re-derive `GRID_DEPTH_RANGE` through a reusable `water_zs`-parameterized helper (D-19.4-12), and
prove at the source level that E1, E3, E5 and E7 are inert (SC-3).

Purpose: post-fix, `max(water_zs) == height_above_water` at every seed, so the clearance floor is
seed-invariant by construction. D-19.4-15 measured this over 3,000 draws (500 seeds x {8,12,16}
cameras x calibration and holdout): exactly one distinct derived floor, **1.176215948246**. The
old constant 1.181852154 was anchored on `max(water_zs) = 1.031 + 5.636 mm` — cam7's seed-42
jitter, the camera whose water surface sat deepest. A module constant is now *correct*; deriving
per-scenario is better hygiene and costs nothing.

Output: `derive_grid_depth_range(water_zs, board, rotation_range_deg)` beside
`default_xy_extent_for_layout`, `GRID_DEPTH_RANGE` computed by calling it once at import, an
exact-value test, and a source-level inertness proof for the four unaffected experiments.

**Do NOT implement the superseded `max(GRID_DEPTH_RANGE[0], derived)` form** (cancelled
D-19.4-02) or the resolved-absolute-range checkpoint payload (cancelled D-19.4-03). They existed
to preserve seed-42 bit-inertness, which this phase deliberately gives up.

**Do NOT touch `board_clearance_floor`'s `margin_factor`** — explicitly out of scope, and tuning
it to make a case pass is blocking anti-pattern 5.
</objective>
