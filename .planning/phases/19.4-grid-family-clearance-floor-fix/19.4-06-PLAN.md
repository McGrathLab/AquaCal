Wire a `--seeds` mode into E1 emitting a committed `exp1_band.csv` over the depth-generalization
territory (D-19.4-14, SC-5a).

Purpose: D-19.4-14 names two E1 findings that MF-08 cites and that currently live only in
gitignored `seed_sweep_19_3/` output — the 97-178x ratio spread and "2 of 10 seeds exceed 2 mm".

author cannot defend.

recorded in E1's two provenance sidecars.

re-litigate it:** the band covers `exp2_depth_generalization.csv`'s territory — the per-depth,
per-model rows. E1 has 8 `TEST_DEPTHS` and 2 `MODELS`, so a 10-seed band is
`10 x 8 x 2 = 160` rows. It does NOT band `exp1_parameter_errors.csv` or
`exp3_xy_vs_z_anisotropy.csv`. If reading the code shows the depth-generalization frame has a
different natural row shape, use the code's shape and record the correction in the SUMMARY — do
not silently band a different CSV.

**E1 is INERT under this phase's interface fix** — its production `SCENARIO_NAME = "realistic"`
resolves to `generate_real_rig_array()` and never reaches `generate_camera_array`. The band exists
for reproducibility, not because E1's numbers move.

**This plan MUST NOT run the production 10-seed band (~57 min).** That is stage 6 of the queue in
plan 09. Verify with a 2-seed band under `--smoke` and a scratch `--out`.
