**User's choice:** Free text — *"I lean towards fixing now, what is the real cost?"*
**Notes:** The question was answered with a measurement rather than an estimate. Instrumenting all
22 rows showed the fix touches 2 of 4 assertions (`r_q_m`, 6/22 rows, worst 1 ULP;

too loose, and the docstring's warning about tolerances passing a drifted formula is fair).
