**Expected end-state counts (D-29.2-10 supersedes D-29-15):**
`pytest tests/ -m "not slow"` → **0 failed**. `pytest tests/` → **exactly 2 failed**, both
`@pytest.mark.slow`, both reading constants from `tests/fixtures/`. Zero and three are both
anomalies after this phase.

---

## Sampling Rate

- **After every task commit:** `pytest tests/ -m "not slow"` — the CI `test` job's own command.
- **After every wave:** the full `pre-commit run --all-files` proof plus all 7 `--smoke` invocations.
- **Phase gate (before the author touches Merge):** the complete clone rehearsal —
  merge simulation → `--print == 2.1.0` → full `semantic-release version` in the clone → inspect
  the resulting commit, tag, `CHANGELOG.md` diff, and sdist member list.
- **Max feedback latency:** ~300 s for the quick command.
