# Phase 29.2 Plan 03: D4 Step Bounds and the CI Pre-Measurement Summary

The one D4 member CI's not-slow selection runs now passes under a bound of 4 and 8 representable
steps with the vectorized-versus-scalar mechanism written at the assertion, and every check the
release PR's `test` and `experiments-smoke` jobs will run was measured here first: not-slow 0
failed, whole suite exactly 2 failed, 7/7 smoke invocations exit 0.

## What Was Built

**Task 1 — the assertion conversion** (`3026813`, `tests/unit/test_optim_common.py`)

`TestPerObservationDetailSinks::test_detail_sink_recomputed_geometry_matches_projector` had four
assertions. Two of them — `r_q_m` and `chord_incidence_deg` — now compare through
`np.testing.assert_array_max_ulp` at `maxulp=4` and `maxulp=8`. The other two, `h_c_m` and `h_q_m`,
measured zero mismatches across all 22 rows and were left at exact equality: loosening them would
discard information for nothing.

A module-level `_assert_max_ulp(actual, desired, maxulp, row)` resolves the message-convention
problem rather than dropping it. `assert_array_max_ulp` accepts no message argument, yet every
assertion in this method carries the row dict, so the helper delegates and on `AssertionError`
re-raises with numpy's own message — which names the observed step count — joined to the row. A
reader of a failure sees both the bound and what was actually measured. The helper returns the
per-element step array so a future caller can record it.

The method docstring was rewritten to the five-part house shape borrowed from
`tests/unit/test_datasets.py:958-973` (documentation shape only; that analog's `rtol` mechanism is
explicitly rejected here). It states what the comparison governs, the 22-row measurement with the
disagreement pair quoted verbatim, the mechanism **as a correction of the open todo**, why 4 and 8
in orders of magnitude both ways, and an instruction to the next reader against both widening the
bound and "fixing" it by vectorizing the test's own recomputation.

## Key Implementation Details

**The arithmetic that makes the delta exact rather than plausible.** The todo's recorded before-state
was `3 failed, 2394 passed, 21 skipped` (2418 collected). Today's measurement is `2 failed, 2395
passed, 21 skipped` (2418 collected). The collected total is unchanged and the passed count is
exactly one higher — one test moved from failed to passed and nothing else moved. That is what "no
test was skipped, xfailed or deselected" looks like as a number rather than as a promise, and it is
stronger evidence than the do-not-silence claim itself.

**Why a step count and not a tolerance.** One representable step corresponds to a *relative* size
anywhere in `[eps/2, eps]` depending on position within the binade, so any single `rtol` is up to 2x
loose or 2x tight — and being 2x looser than intended is precisely the failure the original
docstring existed to prevent. `assert_array_max_ulp`'s bound is inclusive (`ret <= maxulp`), its
default is already `1`, and it returns the observed per-element gap.

**Why 4 and 8 and not 1 and 2.** A bound equal to this machine's measured maximum would be pinned to
this machine by construction — the exact mistake, given that the diagnosis IS that the gap is a
dispatch artifact and CI's runners are ones this project does not control. The assertion remains
extraordinarily tight: at the measured magnitude, 8 steps is 1.110e-16 absolute and 1.04e-15
relative, roughly nine orders of magnitude tighter than the `pytest.approx` default this comparison
must never become.

**Why the chord bound is exactly twice the radial one.** `chord_incidence_deg` is derived from
`r_q` through `arctan2` and `degrees`, so its measured worst case of 2 steps is one inherited step
plus at most one rounding in each call. The measurement agreeing with the mechanism is what makes
the diagnosis credible rather than merely plausible.

`pytest tests/ -m "not slow" --cov=aquacal --cov-report=xml --cov-report=term`. The three coverage

pass/fail outcome. The *selection* — the thing under measurement — is identical. Recorded explicitly
in §3 of the evidence file rather than silently.

**The not-slow BEFORE cell is a labelled derivation, not a fresh run.** Taking it properly would have
meant a second ~20-minute full selection at `HEAD~1`. It rests instead on two measurements that were
taken: the target node executed at `HEAD~1` reported `1 failed` at exit 1 (transcript recorded), and

and `test_pipeline.py:2332`), so neither can contribute to that selection. The evidence file labels
the cell a derivation rather than presenting it as a run.

**Task 2's two pytest verify gates were evaluated against the captured transcripts** of the same
verbatim commands rather than re-run, which would have been a ~50-minute duplicate of a suite the
prepush audit established as deterministic ("re-run in isolation they reproduce bit-for-bit"). The
five cheap gates — the seven smoke invocations, the `experiments/` diff, both md5 anchors and the
evidence-file grep — were re-executed live, so the seven smoke paths were in fact run twice and
exited 0 both times with the tree clean after each.
