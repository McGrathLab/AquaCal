<objective>
ROADMAP criterion 2, second half, and criterion 4.

D-05: after the dry run, **one short real stage** and the **gate roll-up**, on the Linux target.
The short stage must be `fd_jacobian` — `suite_expectations.json` gives it
`depends_on: ["preflight"]` and `est_hours 0.05`. It is the ONLY dependency-free short stage:
`e3` looks cheap at 0.005 h but carries `depends_on: ["e2_production"]` from 26-12's edge, and
`e2_production` is 0.8-1.45 h, so scheduling `e3` drags E2 in behind it.

Then the `--smoke` pass on the target, which is the phase's closing signal: with plans 27-03 and
27-04 having cleared all 12 pre-existing reds, **a red here is a Linux-specific failure and
nothing else.** That is the entire reason D-20 was in scope.

**This plan models the failure case honestly.** D-03: a defect found during verification means
fix -> commit -> next tag -> re-verify, and abandoned tags stay as the audit trail. D-04: a
second tag is a normal outcome of finding a defect, not a process artifact — but nothing forces
one. Do not treat a first-attempt pass as the expected path, and do not pre-build an "attempt 2"
plan either; Task 3 is the loop.

Purpose: prove the package needs no further code edits once transferred, or find out that it does
while it is still cheap.
Output: a completed on-target record and a closed attempt log.
</objective>
