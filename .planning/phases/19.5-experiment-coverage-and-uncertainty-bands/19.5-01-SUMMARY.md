## Accomplishments
- `predict_jacobian_shape()` added to `experiments/e3_derived_quantities.py`: derives `(n_residuals, n_params)` by pure counting from `build_jacobian_sparsity`'s own parameter-block layout (tilt, extrinsics, water_z, board pose, intrinsics blocks), proven exact against the real array over all six `CPR_CONFIGS` entries crossed with both interface modes (12 cases), plus two configurations outside `CPR_CONFIGS` ((20,50) and (24,100)) so the agreement is not coincidental.
- `build_structural_scaling_df()` sweeps `n_cameras` in `[3, 4, 6, 8, 12, 13, 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128]` x `n_frames` in `[50, 100, 200]` x `refine_intrinsics` in `{False, True}` (84 rows total), at the production `normal_fixed=False, shared_interface=True` configuration.
- `experiments/results/structural_scaling.csv` committed as a new, separate artifact. `cpr_grouping.csv`/`cpr_grouping.tex` verified byte-unchanged via `git diff --stat` before and after the full-run write.
- The group-count pinning claim COV-01 must substantiate is demonstrated across the entire computed range: every `record_source="computed"` row with `refine_intrinsics=False` has `n_groups == 13`; every such row with `refine_intrinsics=True` has `n_groups == 17`.
- The 500,000,000-element dense/sparse boundary is located as a disclosed row-level fact: the sweep straddles it at exactly one row, `(n_cameras=128, n_frames=200, refine_intrinsics=True)` (`jacobian_elements=507,289,600`, `exceeds_dense_threshold=True`); the adjacent `(128, 200, refine_intrinsics=False)` row (`402,432,000`) stays under it.

## Decisions Made
- The 200,000,000-element allocation cap was chosen deliberately below the library's 500,000,000-element dense/sparse threshold so the sweep never risks an OOM while still landing `computed` rows on both sides of the disclosed boundary via the proven predictor.
- `structural_scaling.csv` is written only from E3's full (non-smoke, non-check) run path. `--check`/`--smoke` were left untouched, matching the plan's stated scope and avoiding any change to `cpr_grouping.csv`/`.tex`'s existing resumability behavior.
- Measured wall time: `build_structural_scaling_df()` alone took ~6.1 s; the full `python -m experiments.e3_derived_quantities` invocation (including the existing tier 1/2/3 recomputation, all skipped-on-write since baselines already existed) took ~12.3 s. Both well under the 120 s acceptance ceiling.
- Largest `(n_cameras, n_frames)` pair that stayed `record_source="computed"` for both `refine_intrinsics` values: `(64, 200)` (`jacobian_elements` 161,894,400 / 188,108,800, both under the 200,000,000 cap). `(96, 200, *)` and `(128, 200, *)` fell to `predicted`.
- The 500M-element boundary falls between `(128, 200, refine_intrinsics=False)` at 402,432,000 (under) and `(128, 200, refine_intrinsics=True)` at 507,289,600 (over) -- the sweep's only `exceeds_dense_threshold=True` row.
