<design_decision>
**A variant differs from the source in exactly two keys: the top-level `seed:` and
`paths.output_dir`.** The validation strategy asked for "the seed line alone", and that is not
survivable: `paths.output_dir` points at
`Desktop/Aqua/AquaCal/release_calibration`, the directory holding the calibration that produced the
manuscript's Section 3 numbers. Leaving it unchanged would have every band run overwrite the
published release calibration. Retargeting it is required, and the test asserts the diff is
confined to those two keys and nothing else — which is a stronger, checkable statement than "only
the seed".

**Variants are written to a band directory outside the release tree** and are not committed;
their content is fully reproducible from the source config plus the seed, and the provenance
sidecar records the source config's own hash. Never write into
`Desktop/Aqua/AquaCal/release_calibration/`.

**Seed list: 42, 43, 44.** Seed 42 reproduces the current committed record, which buys a free
within-queue reproducibility check against `real_rig_metrics.json` at no extra stage. Three runs at
48-87 min is ~3.5 hours nominal. D-19.5-05 already narrows this band's claim to split variance, and
a modest n is defensible for that narrower claim — but the artifact must state `n=3` and report a
range, not a confidence interval.
</design_decision>
