# Phase 19.5 Plan 11: Manuscript Findings Summary

The phase's point was never the CSVs. It was establishing what may be *claimed*. This plan turns
seven bands into seven findings, each naming a path a reader can regenerate from.

## Seven new entries

| entry | what it establishes | discharges |
|---|---|---|
| **MF-11** | Accuracy improves from N=8 to N=12 (~6 sd) then **plateaus**; N=12 and N=16 indistinguishable (~0.4 sd). R1.3's first measured answer. | COV-03, COV-04 |
| **MF-12** | The line layout's 18.9 mm surface error is ~80% global datum shift; the real standoff error is ~2.4 mm (4x grid) and invisible to every accuracy metric | (from COV-03's band) |
| **MF-13** | Index-induced scale bias stays below the holdout noise floor across seeds 42-47; the reconstruction effect of +/-0.01 is ~5x below seed noise | COV-05 |
| **MF-14** | A ~1.85x wall-clock spread at **identical nfev** -- an empirical noise floor at constant work | COV-06 |
| **MF-15** | E2's band is split variance on fixed data, NOT measurement variance; seed 42 reproduces the committed record exactly | COV-07 |
| **MF-16** | E1's band is regenerable at last, MF-08's artifact citation is corrected, and the "upper bounds" caveat is retired | — |
| **MF-17** | E7's `fixed` arms are vacuous, not null -- undefined by construction, and not evidence of absence | COV-08 (part) |

## The five judgement calls, as decided

1. **E6's accuracy-vs-N stated at full strength** — "improves to 12, then plateaus" — but explicitly
   as a measurement, with no mechanism claimed.
2. **`layout/line` gets its own entry.** It is the most negative finding in the band and folding it
   into MF-11 would have made it read as a caveat on good news. Chasing it produced the phase's
   most surprising result — most of the error is gauge, not geometry.
3. **E5's accuracy claim is restored**, in exactly one bounded sentence: *index-induced scale bias
   remains below the holdout noise floor across seeds 42-47 (n=6)*. It must not become "the method
   is insensitive to refractive index". This wording is also what the roadmap cites for deferring
   Phase 20.
4. **The abstract keeps `~135x`**, defended as a ratio of arm means (135.4x, n=10) rather than seed
   42's 128.1x. The stale `257 mm` becomes `~229 mm (n=10, range 199-252)`.
5. **Per-depth error bars go on both figures**, captioned as calibration-scenario variance against a
   fixed test set.

## What changed while writing this plan

**MF-12 was rewritten after a measurement contradicted its first draft.** The band reports an
18.9 mm `water_z_error_mm_mean` for `layout/line` at seed 43. `water_z_error_mm_mean` is a mean
**absolute** error, so it cannot say whether the surface error and the camera-Z error add or
cancel. Re-solving the configuration and reading signed values settled it: both are negative and
nearly equal, and the physical gap `h_c = water_z - C_z` is off by only 0.36 mm in the mean.
**Roughly 80% of the apparent camera-Z error is a global datum offset** — gauge correction removes
79.5% of it for the line layout against 4.6% for the grid baseline at the same seed, and the real
residual standoff error is ~2.4 mm against grid's ~0.6 mm. This is a defect the library already
knows about — `compute_per_camera_errors` takes a `gauge_correct_z` flag documented as removing
"an artifact of choosing where Z=0 is, not a real geometric error" — and which **E6 does not
pass**.

That is a materially different finding from the one the first draft carried, and it arrived only
because the metric's sign was chased rather than assumed.

**Two framings were corrected mid-plan.** The `layout/line` spread was first described as "15x, the
worst in the band"; ranked by ratio it is only sixth, because five index configurations have
near-zero minima that inflate a ratio meaninglessly. By magnitude it is the clear outlier at 7.5x
the next-worst config's worst case. And E1's "upper bounds" caveat was carried into plan 10's
SUMMARY before MF-08 was re-read; the guard count is a proven null direction and the caveat is
unnecessary.

## Honesty check (Task 3)

- **Every band names a committed path.** Verified per entry; the one that could not (E1's) was
  repaired by quick task 260807-dcv before this plan was written.
- **Every claim carries its n and its limit.** E5's is bounded to a +/-0.010 sweep at one geometry;
  E1's carries the extrapolation and fixed-test-set caveats; E2's carries its split-variance scope.
- **The nulls survived to prose.** MF-13 (index insensitivity), MF-14 (no runtime attribution),
  MF-17 (vacuous arms), MF-12 (the line layout's failure) are all written as findings.
- **No optimality figure exceeds one significant figure**, anywhere in the new entries.
- **No runtime figure is attributed to a code change.**
- **Where no band was obtained, the entry says so** — E7's re-run happened; E4/E6's per-metric
  limits are stated rather than elided.
