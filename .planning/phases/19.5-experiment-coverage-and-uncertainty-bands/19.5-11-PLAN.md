<binding_rules>
These are the rules that decide whether this plan's output is honest. Every one of them has been
violated at least once in this project's history.

1. **D-19.3-17 / D-19.5-06 — a band earns a claim, nothing else does.** A single-seed before/after
   supports no accuracy claim however plausible the delta and however clean the mechanism looks.
   Where a band was not obtained, the entry says "no accuracy claim, and here is why". That is a
   passing outcome.
2. **D-19.5-05 — scope in the entry, not only in the artifact.** E2's band bounds **split variance
   on fixed data, NOT measurement variance**. COV-08's bootstrap bounds **metric sampling variance
   only** and is never presented as a calibration band. COV-05's seed band must be distinguished
   in the text from E5's pre-existing `n_assumed_band`, which varies the assumed index at one seed.
3. **State n, and state the p-value convention.** MF-05's p-values are one-sided (0.00098 = 2^-10)
   and reading them as two-sided has already caused confusion once. Every new p-value says which
   it is. A five-seed unanimous sign test is p = 2^-5 = 0.031 one-sided; say so.
4. **Optimality to one significant figure, never finer.** It varies ~2x run to run.
5. **`nfev` ships beside every wall-clock figure** (MF-03).
6. **The ~2x slowdown remains an open question.** Never write that a fix made calibration faster
   or slower.
7. **Nulls are results** (D-19.5-07). If COV-08's E7 `refined` arm came back with no degeneracy
   signature, that is the entry. Do not soften it, do not omit it, do not retry.
8. **Measure the noise floor before attributing anything.** MF-08 records two occasions where a
   decomposition of pure noise nearly reached the manuscript; a third is the project's most
   recurrent error. Before attributing any movement to a cause, name the band that bounds the
   noise.
</binding_rules>
