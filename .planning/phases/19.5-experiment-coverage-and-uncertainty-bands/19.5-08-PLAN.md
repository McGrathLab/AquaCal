<design_decision>
**The repeat subset is the three 100-frame cells: (8, 100), (12, 100), (16, 100).** Claude's
discretion per CONTEXT.md. Two reasons, both load-bearing:
1. These are exactly the cells MF-03's runtime-inversion finding rests on, so a repeat here tests
   the claim that is actually made.
2. The 200-frame cells are the `near_physical_ceiling` ones (11.3 GiB peak on a 15.7 GiB box, 72%
   of RAM). Repeating those risks an OOM that would abort the whole overnight queue for a number
   nobody is quoting. E4's own docstring measures 8x100 at 948 s and 16x100 at 955 s, so the three
   cells are ~48 minutes nominal — a small, safe stage.

**Run 1 is the committed `benchmark_grid.csv`, not a fresh run.** It was produced at `0ffbe15`
under a frozen sha and is the published record. Re-running it would spend 48 minutes to obtain a
number we already have, and would replace the published one. The splice reads the committed CSV as
`repeat=1` and the queue's second directory as `repeat=2`.
</design_decision>
