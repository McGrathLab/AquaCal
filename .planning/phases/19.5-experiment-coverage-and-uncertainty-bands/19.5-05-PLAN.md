<naming_hazard>
E5 already has a function called `run_band`. It bands the **assumed refractive index** at a fixed
seed. D-19.5-05 exists partly because these two bands are easy to confuse, and that confusion is
what demoted E5's accuracy claim in the first place. Name the new one `_run_seed_band`, name the
artifact `index_sensitivity_seed_band.csv`, and make the sidecar carry both `n_assumed_band` and
`seeds` so an artifact can never be read as the wrong band.
</naming_hazard>
