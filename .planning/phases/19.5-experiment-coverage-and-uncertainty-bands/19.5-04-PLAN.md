<design_decision>
**The resampling unit is the frame, not the row.** An iid bootstrap over the 7,762 rows treats
correlated observations as independent and understates the interval. Resample the 52 `frame_idx`
groups with replacement and recompute the statistic over the concatenated rows of the drawn
groups. This is the single most important property of this plan and a test must assert it directly
(see Task 1).

**Hand-rolled loop, not `scipy.stats.bootstrap`.** The clustered resample is ~15 lines and the
cluster structure is the whole point; `scipy.stats.bootstrap` expresses it less directly. This is
not a "don't hand-roll" violation — that guidance is about Newton solves and sparsity colorers.

**Placement in `experiments/`, per plan 03's design decision** (COV-09 needs a citable committed
artifact).
</design_decision>
