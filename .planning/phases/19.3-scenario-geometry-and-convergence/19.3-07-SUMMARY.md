**The production gate is exactly `count > 0`.** A non-zero
`discard_stats["degenerate_observations_at_solution"]` downgrades a cell's status from `ok`
(or `skipped_existing`) to a new `degenerate` value in `build_grid_dataframe`. `STATUS_VALUES`
becomes `{"ok", "degenerate", "failed", "skipped_existing"}`.

Metric columns stay populated for a `degenerate` row rather than being nulled. That is
deliberate: the row remains analysable, while the status makes it unambiguous that its
reprojection RMS and optimality cannot be read as evidence of convergence.

## The gating split — a design decision worth reviewing

**E4 and E6 gate. E1, E5 and E7 record only.**

The executor's reasoning: E1's `exp1/exp2/exp3` CSVs are fixed byte-identical-header contracts
that an external, read-only figures repository consumes (D-19). They have no `status` column and
adding one would break that consumer. Rather than invent a fourth status vocabulary, the count is
recorded in each benchmark record's `problem_shape.degenerate_observations_at_solution` and a
non-zero value logs one prominent warning naming it; the pass/fail decision moves to plan
19.3-08's queue gate script, where it stays machine-checkable.

This is defensible and keeps the fixed contracts intact, but it is a different shape from
"the harnesses GATE" as D-19.3-11 phrases it — for three of the six experiments the gate lives in
the queue rather than the harness. **Flagged for human review**; it does not block the re-run,
because plan 08's gate script covers those three.

E1's `--smoke` reports **12** guard observations on `create_scenario("ideal")` and still exits 0.
That count is the **extreme-obliquity condition, not a breached interface** — `ideal` measures
0 of 1760 corners at or above the surface with 123.4 mm of clearance. It is a 4-camera array at
0.1 m spacing viewing a 0.6 × 0.42 m board at 1.15 m+, so its outermost corners sit at incidence
angles the refractive model cannot solve. See `19.3-ORCHESTRATOR-NOTES.md` §4.

In E1's case the carve-out is structural rather than conditional: nothing in that module compares
the count to anything, so it can never become an exit code.
