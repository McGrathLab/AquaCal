## The post-fix reproduction count

**8 of 308 cells moved between repeats, before 63 of 308.** Thirteen of fourteen configurations
reproduced exactly; all movement is in `index/1.48`. On the full post-fix schema (25 columns,
including the optimality and guard columns the pre-fix pair could not carry): 10 of 350.

Computed by the same code path that produced the pre-fix figure. `determinism_probe.py --report`
runs a self-test that re-derives **63/308 from the archived pre-fix pair before reporting
anything** — without that pin the two numbers would not be comparable and the measurement would be
void.

This is a **reported statistic, not a gate** (D-19.3-14). There is no tolerance in it and nothing
to loosen. It is reported whatever it shows.

**The supporting cross-tabulation has no post-fix counterpart.** The pre-fix analysis tabulated
movement against per-configuration degenerate-observation count (correlation 0.19, no threshold
rule). Post-fix the guard count is **zero for all fourteen configurations**, so the predictor has
no variance and the correlation is **undefined**. That is reported as undefined rather than dressed
up as an improvement.

## MF-01: before/after, and whether its prose edit changed

Every number in MF-01's table was read from a pre-fix `newton_iterations.csv`. Plan 09 regenerated
it on the corrected geometry.

| quantity | pre-fix | post-fix |
|---|---|---|
| iterations, median | 4.0 | 4.0 (unchanged) |
| iterations, max | 7 (6-7 per camera) | **6** (6 on every camera) |
| incidence angle range | 0.13 - 62.92 deg | **0.08 - 57.50 deg** |
| points | 104,052 | **105,600** |
| not converged | 0 | 0 |

**The prose edit is still required — unchanged in kind, smaller in magnitude.** The understatement
against the supplement's "two to four steps" is now 4 → 6 rather than 4 → 7, so a reader sizing a
compute budget under-provisions by **1.5x** rather than 1.75x. The median is still 4.0 and zero
points fail to converge, so the supplement's typical case remains correct and only its tail is
understated. The conclusion was **restated, not softened** — MF-01 now explicitly warns against
silently swapping 7 for 6, because the batch loop's all-points termination rule means production's
per-point cost is the batch *max*, not its median.

The point count rise to 105,600 = 12 cameras x 100 frames x 88 corners means **every corner is now
valid**; pre-fix, 129 corners per camera were dropped as unprojectable. That is the depth-clearance
fix showing up directly in this file.

The pre-fix table is preserved verbatim beneath the current one and the archived CSV is named
(`experiments/archive/e3-2026-08-02-pre-depth-fix/newton_iterations.csv`), so the two versions are
traceable rather than one silently replacing the other.

## MF-08's E4/E6 subsection was corrected at the checkpoint

Four statements written from a partial model of the failure were falsified by
`.planning/debug/e6-seed-locked-clearance-floor.md` and corrected before close (`0054cd9`):

- "E4 and E6 CANNOT be seed-swept as shipped" → E6 can; **29 of seeds 0-499** clear both draws.
- "Only seed 42 yields a complete E6 sweep" → seeds **62** and **28** both returned `{'ok': 14}`.
- "every configuration builds its own floor" → the floor is **invariant to layout and spacing**
  (180 arrays); it varies with `n_cameras`.
- "the `depth_range=None` fix is verified inert at seed 42" → inert for the **calibration** scene
  only; it shifts the **holdout** scene by up to **0.469 mm**.

E6's verdict is unchanged — still no accuracy claim. What changed is the reason: **no band has been
measured, not that none can exist.**

Two seed-fragile spots were recorded: `scale/double_scale` optimality (elevated on the intrinsic
pass at both non-42 seeds — a convergence-*diagnostic* fragility with no accuracy cost) and
`layout/line` parameter recovery (~4x spread while reconstruction stays clean — the documented
weak-observability signature). Both were invisible while E6 was single-seed.
