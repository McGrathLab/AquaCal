## Three things to check before launching

1. **The E1/E5/E7 gating split.** Plan 07's executor made E4 and E6 gate on the guard count via
   their per-cell `status`, but had E1, E5 and E7 **record only** — their CSVs are fixed
   byte-identical-header contracts consumed by an external read-only figures repo, so adding a
   status column would break that consumer. The pass/fail for those three therefore lives in
   `check_rerun_gates.py` rather than the harness. Defensible, and the gate script does cover
   them, but it is a different shape from "the harnesses GATE" as D-19.3-11 phrases it.

2. **E3 was added to scope after planning** (Amendment A, user-approved 2026-08-02). It was
   absent from the seed's blast-radius table but calls `generate_real_rig_trajectory`, so its
   `newton_iterations.csv` moved by 134 cells — and that file is the evidence behind **MF-01**.
   Only tier 2 should move; tiers 1 and 3 must be **confirmed** unmoved, and movement in either
   is a finding, never something to regenerate away.

3. **`k = 0.1` must never be tuned.** Realised clearance (71.1 mm on the grid family) comfortably
   exceeds the safety factor's guaranteed minimum (~13 mm) because the worst-case tilt did not
   co-occur with the shallowest sampled depth. That is the bound being conservative as designed —
   **not** evidence `k` could be reduced.
