**Locked:** the gate is written on the recorded guard count and on optimality, never on
reprojection RMS — RMS agrees with a broken solve and is a secondary sanity number only (19.2
anti-pattern #2). Never quote optimality beyond one significant figure in any prose or log line
you add; it varies ~2x run-to-run.

**Locked (measured, not assumed): the gate applies to production runs only.** After waves 1-2 all
six production scenarios measured **zero** corners at or above the water surface, with 71-207 mm of
clearance — so a non-zero guard count on a production run is a genuine finding and must fail.
But `create_scenario("ideal")`, which is reachable only from `--smoke`, legitimately trips the
guard with **12 observations** while measuring **0 of 1760** corners above the interface at
123.4 mm clearance. Its cause is the warning's *other* documented condition — corners the
refractive model cannot project for reasons unrelated to being above water. `ideal` is a 4-camera
array at 0.1 m spacing viewing a 0.6 x 0.42 m board at 1.15 m+, so its outermost corners sit at
extreme obliquity. `--smoke` runs in CI; applying the production gate to it unchanged would make CI
report a false failure. See `19.3-ORCHESTRATOR-NOTES.md` section 4.
