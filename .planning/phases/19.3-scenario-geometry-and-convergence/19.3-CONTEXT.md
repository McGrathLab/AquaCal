## Implementation Decisions

### Depth clearance rule

- **D-19.3-01: The clearance bound is derived, and the derivation is the exact one.**
  Use `R_max · sin(θ_normal)` where `R_max = √(half_x² + half_y²)` and `θ_normal` is the
  maximum tilt of the board normal. For a Rodrigues vector `[rx, ry, rz]` with
  `|rx|, |ry| ≤ θ`, the normal tilts by up to `θ√2` — **not** `θ`. The seed's
  `(half_x + half_y)·sin(θ)` form coincides numerically for this board
  (147.5 vs 147.6 mm) by accident of its aspect ratio and must not be assumed general.
  Half-extents are ChArUco **inner-corner** extents, `(squares_x−1)·square_size/2` and
  `(squares_y−1)·square_size/2` — 0.330 × 0.240 m for the 12×9 × 0.060 board — because
  detections are corners, not board edges.

  Validated against measurement: shallowest board centre sits 80.4 mm below the shallowest
  `water_z`, worst observed protrusion is 66.1 mm above it → **146.5 mm of observed upward
  corner reach against a 147.5 mm predicted maximum.** The bound is correct and tight.

- **D-19.3-02: Margin is a 10% fractional safety factor on the derived clearance term.**
  `min_depth = max(water_zs) + 1.1 · R_max · sin(θ_normal)`.

  **Framing is load-bearing and must be preserved in the code comment and the manuscript:**
  the *bound* is derived; `k = 0.1` is a declared safety factor applied to a derived
  quantity. This is categorically different from 19.2 anti-pattern #6 ("adding a constant
  that makes a failing case pass") **only so long as `k` is never adjusted in response to a
  failing run.** If a scenario fails the check, the scenario moves — `k` does not. A planner
  or executor who changes `k` to make something pass has committed the anti-pattern.

  Use the **deepest** per-camera `water_z` (1.0367 in the baseline, not the nominal 1.031);
  interface tilt makes `water_zs` per-camera and the constraint binds on the deepest.

- **D-19.3-03: Tilt ranges stay as they are; each generator derives its own floor.**
  `generate_board_trajectory` keeps `rotation_range_deg = 15.0` → floor ≈ **1.199 m**.
  `generate_real_rig_trajectory` keeps `ROTATION_RANGE_DEG = 20.0` → floor ≈ **1.249 m**.
  `depth_range` max stays 2.0, so usable span shrinks from 0.90 m to 0.80 / 0.75 m.
  Rejected: unifying at 15°, because tilt diversity is what conditions the intrinsic pass —
  reducing it would smuggle an observability change in alongside a geometry fix.

- **D-19.3-04: Violation raises `ValueError` at scenario construction.** No clamping, no
  warn-and-continue. The message names both the required floor and the supplied value.
  Warn-and-continue was rejected explicitly: it is what effectively happens today
  (`DegenerateObservationWarning` fires and is ignored), so it relabels the failure rather
  than fixing it.

  Three call sites hardcode the shallow range and will raise until updated —
  `experiments/e3_derived_quantities.py:332`, `experiments/e5_index_sensitivity.py:188`
  and `:468`. That is intended: it forces a deliberate update.

- **D-19.3-05: `board: BoardConfig` becomes a REQUIRED parameter on both generators.**
  Neither takes a board today, so the bound is not computable without a signature change.
  Required (not optional-with-skip) so enforcement can never be silently bypassed — an
  optional path that skips the check is the exact shape that let this defect live.

- **D-19.3-06: Accept a major version bump.** `generate_board_trajectory` is a public
  export (`src/aquacal/datasets/__init__.py:50` and in `__all__`); it gains a required
  parameter and a new failure mode. Commit as `feat!:` and let python-semantic-release cut
  **v2.0.0**.

  **Cross-phase consequence — Phases 21 and 22 must be told.** The milestone is titled
  "v1.9 Publication Prep" and the manuscript, `CITATION.cff`, README and the Zenodo archive
  all name a version. Phase 21 (dataset refresh) and Phase 22 (release cut) both need this
  before they resolve version strings.

### Generator and preset scope

- **D-19.3-07: E6's scale axis anchors at the derived floor, not at the water surface.**

  As written, `_scaled_depth_range` (`experiments/e6_generalization_sweep.py:138-147`)
  scales the standoff *below the water surface*. Its docstring claims this keeps the board
  underwater at every scale factor — true of the board **centre** only. Because
  `square_size` is deliberately not scaled (lines 125-128), halving the standoff halves the
  available clearance while the requirement stays fixed:

  | | standoff below surface | min depth | required floor | verdict |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | baseline today | 0.069 | 1.100 | 1.199 | violates by 99 mm |
  | `half_scale` today | 0.0345 | **1.0655** | 1.199 | violates by 133 mm |
  | `half_scale` after floor → 1.199 | 0.084 | **1.115** | 1.199 | **still violates by 84 mm** |

  For `half_scale` to clear the bound under the current definition the baseline floor would
  have to sit at `1.031 + 2 × 0.168 = 1.367 m`, which abandons the real-rig operating depth
  D-29 established. **So the scale axis and the clearance rule are in direct conflict as
  currently defined, and the axis is what changes.**

  New definition: scale the depth **extent above the derived floor**, keeping the floor
  fixed — `half_scale = (floor, floor + 0.5·(2.0 − floor))`. Every scale value is legal by
  construction. The board-to-surface standoff stops scaling, which is defensible: standoff
  is set by the tank and the target, not by rig size.

  **E6's axis prose must be corrected to match.** The current docstring's "does accuracy
  hold as the whole rig/tank scales up or down together" claim is no longer exactly what the
  axis measures, and leaving the prose stale would be a documented-but-false claim.

  Also noted, for whoever implements it: even scaling the board uniformly would leave
  `half_scale` ~3 mm short, because `_scaled_depth_range` anchors at the uniform
  `GRID_HEIGHT_ABOVE_WATER = 1.031` while the constraint anchors at the deepest per-camera
  `water_z = 1.0367`, and the 5.7 mm of interface tilt does not scale. Anchoring at
  `max(water_zs)` is the general fix regardless of which scaling definition is used.

- **D-19.3-08: Board size is CONSTANT across every scenario. Do not vary `square_size`
  per preset or per scale value.** (User decision, and it overrode the option originally
  offered.)

  Rationale, which downstream agents must not re-litigate: framing is governed by
  `f · board_extent / standoff`. Rig spacing, camera count and layout do not enter it. A
  board correctly sized at 1–2 m stays correctly sized at 1–2 m under any layout, so letting
  `square_size` vary would inject a cross-scenario confound that is invisible in the
  resulting numbers — *"a rabbithole of hard-to-debug nuances."*

  Corollary that follows directly: `ideal` and `minimal` are mis-framed because of their
  **standoff**, not their board. Their cameras sit at Z ≈ 0 with water at 0.15 and the board
  at 0.25–0.45, so camera-to-board is 0.25–0.45 m against a lens framed for 1–2 m — the real
  board already overfills their frame by ~4×, today, before any clearance check.

- **D-19.3-09: Finish the real-rig standoff into the library — presets AND the default.**

  D-29 changed the **experiment layer only**. Verified state before this phase:

  | site | value | changed by D-29? |
  |---|---|---|
  | `experiments/e4_benchmark_grid.py` `GRID_HEIGHT_ABOVE_WATER` | 1.031 | ✅ this is what D-29 did |
  | `src/aquacal/datasets/synthetic.py:136` — `generate_camera_array` default | **0.15** | ❌ no |
  | `synthetic.py:759` — `create_scenario('ideal')` | **0.15** | ❌ no |
  | `synthetic.py:791` — `create_scenario('minimal')` | **0.15** | ❌ no |
  | `create_scenario('realistic')` | 1.031 via `generate_real_rig_array()` | n/a |

  Any note claiming D-29 "replaced the 0.15 m rig" project-wide is true of E4/E6 and only of
  E4/E6.

  Change `generate_camera_array`'s default `height_above_water` 0.15 → 1.031 **and** both
  presets, so the library cannot construct a mis-framed rig by accident. `default_board`
  stays shared and unchanged. Presets then differ only in what should differ: camera count,
  layout, spacing, frame count, noise.

- **D-19.3-10: The preset change moves ZERO publishable numbers — verified, not assumed.**

  | caller | scenario | role |
  |---|---|---|
  | `experiments/e1_refractive_comparison.py:387, :569` | `"realistic"` (1.031) | the real E1 run |
  | `experiments/e1_refractive_comparison.py:477` | `"ideal"` | **`--smoke` only**, temp dir |
  | `experiments/e7_interface_ablation.py:192` | `"minimal" if smoke else "realistic"` | **`--smoke` only** |
  | E3, E5 | `generate_real_rig_array()` | 1.031 |
  | E4, E6 | `GRID_HEIGHT_ABOVE_WATER = 1.031` | D-29 |

  Fallout is confined to: smoke-test numbers, `tests/unit/test_datasets.py:256` (passes 0.15
  explicitly), the `ideal` frozen anchor (already regenerated once under D-27, `4ed8259`),

### Convergence gate

- **D-19.3-11: The library RECORDS; the experiment harnesses GATE.** The library counts
  guard hits on the **final solution evaluation** and records them alongside the existing
  `discard_stats` in `diagnostics.json`; harnesses treat a non-zero count as
  `status != "ok"`, so a degenerate cell can never be published as converged.

  Rejected: raising at solve end, and an opt-in strict flag. Reasoning, recorded because it
  will be re-proposed otherwise:

  1. **The guard firing does not mean the answer is wrong — it means the diagnostic is
     invalid.** Above the surface there is no water between camera and point, so pinhole is
     the *physically correct* model there. The solve is not broken; its optimality is simply
     no longer readable as a convergence measure, because the residual is C0-but-not-C1. The
     right response to an invalidated diagnostic is to record that it is invalid. Raising
     would assert the answer is wrong, which the accuracy evidence contradicts: the
     high-optimality group (0.5938 mm mean reconstruction RMSE) is indistinguishable from the
     healthy group (0.5622 mm), and `half_scale` — optimality 27/140 — has the **best**
     accuracy in the table at 0.4935 mm.
  2. **Inertness.** Count-and-record is provably inert the way 19.2 plan 27's optimality
     capture was (exact-equality tests against the uninstrumented path). Raising alters
     control flow on a path real data can reach, which drags **E2 back into scope** — not
     merely 48–87 min, but re-opening §3 numbers re-verified at 0.000% delta under D-34 one
     day ago, twenty days from deadline. It buys nothing: the defect is in synthetic scenario
     construction, which cannot touch E2's real data.
  3. D-19.3-04's construction-time `ValueError` already catches the case that matters. The
     convergence count catches something narrower — an *estimated* pose or `water_z` putting
     a corner above the *estimated* interface despite a legal ground-truth scenario. That is
     a "the solver wandered" signal, and it belongs in diagnostics.
  4. An opt-in strict flag is D-19.3-11 with a second untested code path; the default-off
     path is what every user gets.

  **"At convergence" means the final residual evaluation, not any evaluation.** During
  iteration the guard legitimately fires — that is the entire reason it exists (the
  `7e0cb90` absorbing-region fix, which must not be re-opened). The recorded count must be
  taken on a solution-state evaluation, distinct from any running count.

- **D-19.3-12: Correct the `DegenerateObservationWarning` text.**
  `src/aquacal/config/schema.py:607-618` currently says *"Check first-order optimality, not
  RMS, when this fires"* — advice that is wrong in precisely the situation that emits it,
  since the pinhole continuation is what inflates optimality. It must say optimality is
  **unreliable** when this fires. Shipped-library defect, independent of everything else in
  this phase.

- **D-19.3-13: Run the full paired determinism sweep.** The whole 14-config E6 sweep runs
  **twice** on corrected geometry, compared against the two pre-fix sweeps already on disk.

  Cost is **+1.8 h, not +3.6** — the production E6 re-run *is* repeat 1 (same code, same
  config, same seed, which is exactly what the comparison needs), so only one additional
  pass is required. Sequence total ≈ **9 h**.

- **D-19.3-14: The pre-declared measure is the CELL REPRODUCTION COUNT, reported as a
  statistic and not as a gate.** "N of 308 cells moved between repeats", before vs after.
  Pre-fix baseline is already measured at **63/308**.

  This does not violate the 19.2 constraint. That constraint forbids bit-identity as a
  **pass/fail gate** on marginally-conditioned configurations; using it as a **reported
  outcome statistic** is a different thing and is exactly what this measurement is for.
  There is no tolerance anywhere, so there is nothing to loosen (anti-pattern #3).

  **Whatever the number is, it is the finding — including if it does not improve.** The
  criterion is declared before launch and is not revisited afterwards. Correlation evidence
  going in is weak (`corr(degenerate count, movement) = 0.19`; every config with ≤2
  degenerate observations reproduced bit-exactly and every one with ≥6 moved, but the
  baseline with 12 also reproduced exactly, so no threshold rule holds). Test at production
  frame counts — anti-pattern #4 (every geometry variant converges at 6 frames *with the bug
  present*; the failure needs ~50).

  `detect-secrets`. Five experiments move at once, which is precisely when git archaeology
  stops being a sufficient audit trail.

- **D-19.3-16: Foreground the correction as a finding in the reviewer response.** Present
  the discovery and the fix explicitly: a benchmark-construction flaw was found via the
  optimality instrumentation the reviewers' own questions prompted, corrected, and shown not
  to have affected accuracy. Turns moved numbers from a liability into evidence of rigour,
  for an audience the seed describes as *"clearly knowledgeable about and interested in the
  optimization complexities."* The alternative — updating numbers with a one-line methods
  note — reads worse to any reviewer who diffs the versions.

  The manuscript tree is **read-only** from this repo. This phase produces prose and tables
  for the user to place; it does not edit the manuscript.

- **D-19.3-17: "Accuracy unaffected" is claimed only where a measured seed band supports
  it.** Per experiment, the table carries the moved headline numbers, the optimality and
  degenerate-count change, and the delta expressed against **that experiment's own measured
  seed band**. E1 (5 seeds), E7 (10 seeds) and E5 (5 seeds) have bands; **E4 and E6 do not**,
  so those two report the optimality/degeneracy improvement **without** an accuracy claim.

  Non-negotiable, because this project has already paid for it twice: a single-seed
  before/after cannot support an accuracy claim. 19.2's "fixed code is worse on 5 of 6
  metrics" table was overturned by measuring the noise floor, and Phase 19.1 recorded the
  same lesson independently — *a plausible delta with a plausible mechanism is not evidence
  anything changed.*

- **D-19.3-18: The re-run sequence is CHAINED with automated gates.** One code-only wave
  first (D-26: all `src` changes land and are proven inert before any run yielding a
  publishable result), then all five experiments queued serially in a single detached
  overnight with machine-checkable gates — non-zero guard count at convergence fails the
  cell, optimality recorded, attribution checks scripted.

  This preserves review H4 (one production run at a time on the box) while collapsing five
  sessions into one night, and it is precedented: 19.2's overnight queue completed waves 4
  **and** 5 unattended, 12/12 stages.

  **Launch detached (`nohup … & disown`), never `run_in_background`** — the harness kills
  background commands at ~35–50 min and this has already cost the project three sweeps.
  **Commit nothing while a run is in flight** — per-cell `git_sha` capture splits an
  artifact's recorded provenance.

### Constraints inherited and NOT reopened

Locked before this discussion (user, 2026-08-01, in `19.3-SEED.md`):

- Fix it properly rather than document around it.
- **Do NOT smooth the hinge with a blend constant.** The C0-not-C1 kink is real physics for
  a flat interface. A blend would introduce an arbitrary width constant (anti-pattern #6) and
  make the residual non-physical inside the band.
- Keep the pinhole continuation. Removing it re-opens the `7e0cb90` defect (undefined/NaN
  residual → zero-gradient absorbing region).
- **`WATER_Z` stays frozen at 1.031.** Do not re-derive it to 1.0738 or to whatever the next
  calibration reports. E4/E6 are coupled to it by D-29.
- **Never quote optimality beyond one significant figure** — it varies ~2× between runs of
  identical code. It supports "1e-3 healthy vs 1e1–1e9 broken", nothing finer.
- **Bit-identity is not a valid pass/fail gate on the recovered parameters of
  marginally-conditioned configurations.** It IS valid on cost, on small problems, and on
  well-conditioned ones.
