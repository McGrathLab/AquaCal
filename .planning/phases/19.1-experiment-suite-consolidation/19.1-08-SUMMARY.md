## Why the gate mattered here

number would cost manuscript prose. The opposite happened: every §3 quantity reproduced at
0.000%. But the gate still earned its cost, for a reason D-16 did not anticipate — **the
first version of the delta table was wrong**, and the gate is what created the space to find
out before anything reached the manuscript.

The wave-3 table reported six of nine quantities as MOVED, each with a named mechanism and a
Δ%. Every one of those comparisons was invalid: it compared a 12-frame validation set against
§3's 52-frame one, and pooled RMS against mean-of-per-camera. Both defects produced
*plausible* numbers with *plausible* explanations.
