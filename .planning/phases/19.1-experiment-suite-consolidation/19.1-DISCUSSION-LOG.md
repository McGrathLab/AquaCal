**Notes:** ⚠️ D-22 is a deliberate softening of P6; determinism is instead asserted by a unit
test on the comparator plus sorted-key CSV writing. E2's smoke must report a visible `SKIPPED`

the failure P7 exists to prevent. Resumability (`--force` + per-config output files) is
implemented now even though only E7's four arms need it here, because 19.2's grids are the real
consumers and retrofitting after a grid exists is the expensive order.
