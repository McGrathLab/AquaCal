<pitfall_3_resolution>
## RESOLVED BY THE PLANNER — what "fixed intrinsics" means in E7, and why

**The problem.** `calibrate_synthetic(refine_intrinsics=False)` sets
`opt_intrinsics = scenario.intrinsics` (`experiment_helpers.py:135-137`) — that is
**ground-truth** intrinsics, not intrinsics independently estimated from an in-air Stage-1
calibration. D-18's framing discipline presents the fixed-intrinsics arm as AquaCal's own rationale
for importing intrinsics from air, so a reviewer could reasonably ask whether GT intrinsics make the
arm cleaner than reality.

**The decision: use ground-truth-fixed intrinsics, and label them as such in the data.**

**Why this is the correct choice rather than a shortcut.** The primary result is a *geometric*
degeneracy: N free `water_z_i` beside N free camera centers reintroduces the `C_z_i ↔ water_z_i`
redundancy, because only the sum `C_z + d` — the absolute surface height — is physically
meaningful and must be common to all cameras. That redundancy exists in the parameterization
regardless of how accurate the intrinsics are. Perturbing the intrinsics would inject a *second*,
unrelated error source (focal error propagating into recovered height) into the arm whose whole
purpose is to isolate the first one, and any resulting scatter could then be attributed to
intrinsic noise rather than to the ablation variable. Fixing intrinsics at ground truth makes the
per-camera arm the **strongest possible case for per-camera mode** — the optimizer is handed exact
intrinsics and *still* scatters the recovered heights. A degeneracy demonstrated under best-case
conditions is a stronger claim than one demonstrated under noise, because noise cannot be blamed
for it. It is also the only choice that keeps all four arms differing in exactly the two ablation
variables.

**What this obliges the implementation to do.** The claim must be visible in the data, not only in
prose, because a code-reading reviewer will check:
  1. A module constant `INTRINSICS_FIXED_SOURCE = "ground_truth"` with a comment carrying this
     reasoning.
  2. An `intrinsics_source` column in `interface_ablation.csv`, valued `ground_truth_fixed` for the
     two fixed arms and `refined_from_ground_truth` for the two refined arms. A reader of the CSV
     alone learns what "fixed" meant.
  3. A paragraph in `experiments/README.md` (written in plan 19.1-07) stating that the
     fixed-intrinsics arm is a best-case bound, not the CalibMar-import scenario, and why a
     best-case bound is the stronger demonstration.
  4. The same statement in the script's module docstring.

**Do NOT** perturb, noise, or re-derive the intrinsics. **Do NOT** call
`calibrate_synthetic(refine_intrinsics=False)` and leave the ambiguity unlabeled — E7 calls
`optimize_interface`/`joint_refinement` directly for its arms, so the intrinsics it passes are
explicit at the call site.
</pitfall_3_resolution>
