# Phase 19.1 Plan 05: E7 Interface Ablation Summary

## The headline result

Four arms, identical scenario/noise/target/seed, GT `water_z` = 1.031 m (a single flat
surface, all cameras at the same height).

| arm | `water_z` spread | `water_z` err | height drift RMS | cond. number | height↔distance corr | RMSE (control) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| per-camera / fixed **(primary)** | **14.24 mm** | −0.92 mm | 3.41 mm | 1,294 | **−0.861 … −0.830** | 0.985 px |
| per-camera / refined | **15.46 mm** | +1.72 mm | 3.16 mm | **6.2 × 10¹⁰** | −0.607 … −0.005 | 0.985 px |
| shared / fixed | 0.00 mm | −0.92 mm | 1.12 mm | **285** | −0.426 … −0.063 | 0.499 px |
| shared / refined | 0.00 mm | +1.19 mm | 0.62 mm | 596,207 | −0.227 … −0.086 | 0.498 px |

**The primary arm (per-camera, fixed intrinsics) demonstrates the height/distance
degeneracy directly**, and it does so in the strongest possible case for per-camera mode:
intrinsics are held at ground truth, so no focal error can propagate. Twelve cameras that
physically share one water surface recover heights spanning **14.24 mm**, with a
per-camera `C_z_i ↔ water_z_i` correlation of **−0.83 to −0.86**. That correlation is
measured, not inferred from scatter.

**Shared mode is 4.5× better conditioned** (285 vs 1,294) and enforces the physical
constraint by construction.

**Per-camera with intrinsics unlocked is numerically near-singular** (condition number
6.2 × 10¹⁰). This is independent support for D-18's framing that importing Stage-1 in-air
intrinsics is AquaCal's design rationale, not an incidental convenience.

**RMSE behaves exactly as a control should.** It moves only 0.5 → 1.0 px between shared
and per-camera — both comfortably sub-pixel — while the physical error moves 0 → 14 mm.
This is the flat-valley claim demonstrated rather than asserted, and it is precisely why
the column is named `reprojection_rms_px_control` rather than presented as evidence.

## The bug this plan caught

**The first run produced the opposite conclusion, and it was wrong.** Initial results showed
the shared arm losing badly (RMSE 6.53 px vs 0.98, height drift 45.9 mm, `water_z` off by
+23 mm) — a result that would have contradicted the manuscript's central claim about the
shared-interface parameterization.

It was a solver failure, not a finding. Diagnostic evidence:
- The shared arm terminated on `xtol` (status 3) with **optimality 1.5 × 10¹¹** — nowhere
  near a stationary point. It stopped because step size collapsed.
- Per-camera terminated cleanly on `ftol` (status 2) at optimality 6.6 × 10³.
- A *constrained* model (shared) cannot legitimately lose this badly to its unconstrained
  parent when the ground truth genuinely satisfies the constraint.

Root cause: `estimate_extrinsics` was called without `water_zs`, `interface_normal`,
`n_air`, and `n_water`, so every arm began from a **non-refractive** Stage-2 initial guess.
That is a Stage-2 defect entirely unrelated to the ablation variable, and it confounded the
comparison the experiment exists to make. Fixed in `f984397`; `initial_water_zs` also now
seeds from the ground-truth per-camera heights rather than their mean, matching
`calibrate_synthetic`'s own pattern.

After the fix the shared arm converges (optimality 6.6 × 10³, cost 3,410 vs 38,584,
`water_z` 1.03008 vs GT 1.031) and the expected narrative holds.

**Lesson for the phase:** E7 has no equivalent of D-16's or D-19's escalation gate. A result
contradicting the paper's central claim was one SUMMARY away from being written up. The
discriminator that caught it was solver-convergence metadata (`optimality`, termination
status), not the science numbers — which looked plausible on their own.

## D-17 / D-18 compliance

- Four arms on identical scenario, noise, target, seed, and `initial_water_zs`.
- `intrinsics_source` column present (`ground_truth_fixed` / `refined_from_ground_truth`),
  so what "fixed intrinsics" means is stated **in the data**, not only in prose (Pitfall 3).
- The failure is labelled **height/distance** (`C_z_i ↔ water_z_i`) throughout the module
  docstring, column names, and this summary — never focal/standoff. `standoff_m` and
  `focal_drift_pct` are recorded columns because D-17 requires them; that is not the same
  as labelling the failure with them.
- Refine-ON arms are framed as AquaCal's own rationale for importing air intrinsics, never
  as "what CalibMar produces".
- Per-camera spread comes from the public `build_interface_spread_report` (promoted in
  wave 1), not duplicated inline statistics.
- Solver diagnostics reach the harness via the existing `diagnostics_out` out-parameter;
  stage keys are the settled `stage3_interface_optimization` / `stage3_intrinsic_pass`.

## Deviations

- **`interface_ablation_conditioning.npz` is gitignored rather than committed.** It is
  3.1 MB of dense correlation matrices (already `savez_compressed`; they do not compress
  further) against the repo's deliberate 1000 KB `check-added-large-files` policy. All
  extracted scientific content — condition number, full singular-value list, the
  height↔distance correlation block, interface-spread stats, parameter names — is in the
  committed `interface_ablation_conditioning.json`. Regenerable via
  `python -m experiments.e7_interface_ablation --force` (~7 min). This deviates from the
  plan's declared artifact list; flagged for review.
- **This plan was completed by the orchestrator.** Its executor stopped three times while
  waiting on background work and returned without committing outputs or writing a SUMMARY.
  The harness, the arms, and the Stage-2 fix are the executor's work; the orchestrator
  diagnosed the non-convergence, verified the fix by re-running all four arms, secured the
  artifacts, and wrote this summary.
