<compute_xyz_errors_placement>
## RESOLVED BY THE PLANNER — where the notebook's inline `compute_xyz_errors` lands

Notebook cell `jq300wte3tn` defines `compute_xyz_errors(calibration, test_poses, test_detections,
board)` inline: it triangulates corners via the already-public `triangulate_charuco_corners` and
decomposes the reconstruction error into XY and Z components. RESEARCH calls its placement
borderline between the library and the experiment layer.

**Decision: port it into `experiments/e1_refractive_comparison.py`, not into `pipelines.py`.**

Reasoning: P2's purpose is to prevent two implementations of one scientific quantity, and either
placement yields exactly one implementation. What distinguishes them is API surface — promoting it
to `aquacal.datasets.pipelines` would mean designing, documenting, and supporting a public function
whose only caller is E1's anisotropy analysis, in a phase working against a 2026-08-21 deadline. It
wraps a function that is already public, and its output is a derived statistic specific to one
figure panel.

**What this obliges the implementation to do**, so the choice does not become an untested blind
spot: the ported function carries a docstring naming
`docs/tutorials/02_synthetic_validation.ipynb` cell `jq300wte3tn` as its origin, and it gets direct
unit coverage in `tests/unit/test_experiments_e1.py` (Task 2). It is the only genuinely novel piece
of E1 logic, so it is the piece most likely to be ported wrong, and it must not be validated only
by the 60-minute end-to-end comparison.
</compute_xyz_errors_placement>
