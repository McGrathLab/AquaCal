## Run provenance -- read this before the table

This run used **60 usable frames** total (the Zenodo `real-rig` archive ships pre-extracted
JPEG frames, not full videos -- see `19.1-E2-DATASET-FINDINGS.md`), split by
`holdout_fraction: 0.2` at `seed=42` into **48 calibration frames / 12 validation frames**.
`[Frame Rejection] No outlier frames (median=0.96 px, threshold=5.00 px). No frames dropped.`
-- v1.7's `reject_outlier_frames` mechanism **did not fire** on this run; zero frames were
rejected. 12 primary cameras were located (plus 1 auxiliary fisheye camera, 13 total).

**This is very likely a materially smaller validation set than whatever produced the
committed §3 numbers** (7,762 corner-pair comparisons vs this run's 1,817 -- see row 7). The
reader must not treat row-by-row deltas below as a clean like-for-like comparison of "the same
experiment on two library versions" -- the calibration-frame content may differ from whatever
produced the committed numbers (unknown: whether the committed run used a fuller/original video
capture, of which this archived dataset may be a reduced-frame extraction). Every delta below is
real and reproducible from this exact archived dataset, but part of the movement in the
validation-driven rows (1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7) may be attributable to validation-set size/composition
differences rather than to v1.7/v1.8 code changes alone.

## Manuscript impact

Word budget is at ~3,916 of 4,000 with captions (D-16), so every edit below has a real cost.

- **Row 3 (auxiliary fisheye 15.1 → 25.991 px) is the one number that plausibly needs a
  §3 prose edit or a footnote.** A 72% relative move on a headline number, even one already
  reproduced by the tutorial's own stored output, is large enough that quoting "15.1 px" in the
  manuscript without qualification risks being wrong for the reader who reruns the archived
  dataset. Candidate resolutions (for the wave-6 reviewer, not decided here): (a) update the
  prose to the fresh 25.991 px value and note it as a validation-holdout-sensitive figure, or
  (b) keep 15.1 px but add a footnote citing validation-set-size sensitivity, or (c) investigate
  further before the manuscript is finalized (this delta table does not investigate the root
  cause of the holdout-split sensitivity itself, only documents it).
- **Rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 (the validation-block numbers) may not need individual prose edits**
  if the manuscript's phrasing already treats them as approximate ("~0.9 px", "on the order of
  0.4 mm") -- but if any is quoted to two or more significant figures, the comparability caveat
  above (this run's 12-frame holdout vs the committed run's apparently larger validation set)
  should be resolved (i.e., confirmed whether the committed numbers came from the same
  60-frame archive or a larger unarchived capture) before deciding whether an edit is needed.
- **Rows 8 and 9 (z_w, camera heights) need no manuscript edit.** They were already
  well-approximated by the existing prose and move by noise-level amounts against the
  already-committed CSV.

change to the calibrated camera parameters themselves. This narrows the "MOVED" mechanism for
rows 1, 2, 4, 5, 6, 7 specifically to validation-set composition, not to a drift in the
calibrated extrinsics/intrinsics.
