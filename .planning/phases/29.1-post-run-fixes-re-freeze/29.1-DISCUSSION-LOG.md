## E4 guard-count reporting

| Option | Description | Selected |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Populate 198, exempt pipeline rows from the `>0` downgrade | Publishes the real value so Gate 1 sees a number, without asserting unreliability the library declines to assert | ✓ |

**User's choice:** Populate 198 and exempt pipeline rows from the status downgrade.

**Notes:** Reframed mid-discussion by the user's correction that the 198 are settled — above-water
board corners. That moved criterion 1 from "open scientific question" to "reporting decision", and
made the `degenerate` option less attractive: on measured hardware the library's suggested remedy
(move the board) is unavailable retrospectively, so a re-run reports the same count and any status
chosen here is permanent rather than temporary.

A consequence surfaced during discussion and recorded as D-02: populating the field makes Gate 1
fail on the "non-zero" half of its predicate exactly as the null failed the "missing" half, so the
exemption has to land in `check_rerun_gates.py` as well as `e4_benchmark_grid.py`. Fixing one site
converts one FAIL into a different FAIL.
