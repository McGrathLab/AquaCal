## Deviations from Plan

### Additions (Rule 2 — missing critical content in a manuscript-facing record)

**1. [Rule 2] The seed-sensitivity of the count, which the plan did not anticipate and the closing todo got wrong.**
- **Found during:** Task 1, while checking the todo's claim that 198 appeared "identically" across band seeds.
- **Issue:** `experiments/results_e2_band/seed_{42,43,44}/diagnostics.json` measure **198 / 210 / 183** (0.268 % / 0.284 % / 0.247 %) on this run. The cause decomposition is unanimous on every seed — 100 % `above_interface`, 100 % `extended`, zero everything else — but the *count* is not invariant. The 2026-08-15 claim of identity was about the archive's older band and invited reading 198 as a fixed property of the dataset.
- **Fix:** MF-24 gains a §"The count is seed-sensitive; the cause is not" with the table, and the §3 caveat that 198 must be attributed to seed 42. Left uncorrected, a manuscript sentence could have presented a seed-dependent number as a dataset constant.
- **Commit:** `65294c5`

**2. [Rule 2] The disjoint-frame-set corroboration, which is the strongest evidence in the entry.**
- **Found during:** Task 1, cross-checking `reconstruction_errors.csv` against the flagged frames.
- **Issue:** The plan expected the 31-of-7762 rate to serve as a rate comparison. Measured, it is much more: the calibration and holdout frame sets are **disjoint**, and the two holdout frames with above-surface corners (**21 and 103**) are exactly the frames flanking the two flagged clusters. That is what upgrades "the estimate places these corners in air" to "the board was lifted" — two estimators, two populations, one localized event.
- **Fix:** Its own MF-24 section. It also explains, correctly, why frame 103 carries none of the 198 (it was held out of the solve, not clean) — a point the plan flagged as a caveat and the measurement converts into corroboration.
- **Commit:** `65294c5`

**3. [Rule 2] The surrogate caveat on leg 4 of the TIR refutation.**
- **Found during:** Task 1, reading `experiments/_degeneracy.py:60-63`.
- **Issue:** The plan called the chord-incidence measurement the "decisive" fourth leg. `chord_incidence_deg` is a **straight-chord surrogate, not the refracted exit angle** — for a flagged observation the Newton loop never runs, so no refraction point exists at which to take an angle. Publishing leg 4 as an exit-angle measurement would have put a new version of the *same category error* leg 4's own history warns about into the manuscript pipeline.
- **Fix:** Leg 4 is presented as the plan asked — the fourth leg, replacing rather than repeating the 2026-08-15 argument — with an explicit block stating that legs 1 and 2 are what make the refutation absolute and that leg 4 must not be quoted as a direct exit-angle measurement.
- **Commit:** `65294c5`

**4. [Rule 2] The second half of the "benign" claim.**
- **Found during:** Task 1, reading `_extend_invalid_projections` and the run's own warning text.
- **Issue:** The plan (following the todo) framed the finding as benign because the flagged observations "carry no water-surface gradient". True — `_extend_invalid_projections` (`_optim_common.py:56`) takes only `camera` and `points_3d` and never sees the interface — but stated alone it is misleading: the library's own warning says "every other parameter keeps full gradient", and the extension's docstring names that as the restoring gradient that pushes a lifted board back underwater.
- **Fix:** MF-24 quotes the run's warning verbatim from `e2_production.log:158` and states that "zero gradient" is true of the interface parameters specifically and false of the residual as a whole.
- **Commit:** `65294c5`
